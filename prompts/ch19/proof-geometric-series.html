Geometric Series — Convergence and Sum

Proof with the help of a Large Language Model (AI)

StatementThe geometric series $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a\,r^n$ (with $a\ne 0$) has partial sums $S_N=a\,\dfrac{1-r^{\,N+1}}{1-r}$ (for $r\ne 1$). It converges if and only if $|r|\lt 1$, with sum $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a\,r^n=\dfrac{a}{1-r}$; for $|r|\ge 1$ it diverges. In particular, for $a=1,\ r=\tfrac12$: $S_N=2\bigl(1-\tfrac{1}{2^{\,N+1}}\bigr)\to 2$.
