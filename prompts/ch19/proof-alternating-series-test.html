1. What is proved: the statement in the book and in Lean

  • If $(b_n)$ is decreasing with $b_n\to 0$, then $b_0-b_1+b_2-\cdots$ converges to some $S$, and $|S-S_N|\le b_N$, where $S_N$ is the sum of the first $N$ terms
    theorem leibniz_test (b : ℕ → ℝ) (hb : Antitone b) (hb0 : Tendsto b atTop (𝓝 0)) :
    ∃ S, Tendsto (fun N => ∑ i ∈ Finset.range N, (-1) ^ i * b i) atTop (𝓝 S) ∧
      ∀ N, |S - ∑ i ∈ Finset.range N, (-1) ^ i * b i| ≤ b N

The book writes the series as $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^{n-1}b_n$${}=b_1-b_2+b_3-\cdots$; here the indexing starts at $0$, that is, the series is $b_0-b_1+b_2-\cdots$, with terms $(-1)^i b_i$. The sum of the first $N$ terms is ∑ i ∈ Finset.range N, (-1) ^ i * b i, and the first omitted term is $b_N$, which corresponds to $b_{N+1}$ in the book. A decreasing sequence is written Antitone b. The hypothesis $b_n\gt 0$ is not needed: it follows from monotonicity and $b_n\to 0$ that $b_n\ge 0$. For the bound, the proof splits into two cases, $N$ even or odd, and uses the fact that $S$ lies between two consecutive partial sums: $S_{2k}\le S\le S_{2k+1}$.

2. Where it is in Mathlib

We used the following theorems:

  • Antitone.tendsto_alternating_series_of_tendsto_zero: the alternating series test (Leibniz): the series converges.
  • Antitone.alternating_series_le_tendsto: $S_{2k}\le S$.
  • Antitone.tendsto_le_alternating_series: $S\le S_{2k+1}$.
  • Nat.even_or_odd': every natural number is of the form $2k$ or $2k+1$.

import Mathlib

namespace Ch19.Enallassousa
open Filter Topology

-- the series b₀ - b₁ + b₂ - …, with b decreasing and b n → 0
theorem leibniz_test (b : ℕ → ℝ) (hb : Antitone b) (hb0 : Tendsto b atTop (𝓝 0)) :
    ∃ S, Tendsto (fun N => ∑ i ∈ Finset.range N, (-1) ^ i * b i) atTop (𝓝 S) ∧
      ∀ N, |S - ∑ i ∈ Finset.range N, (-1) ^ i * b i| ≤ b N := by
  obtain ⟨S, hS⟩ := hb.tendsto_alternating_series_of_tendsto_zero hb0
  refine ⟨S, hS, fun N => ?_⟩
  obtain ⟨k, rfl | rfl⟩ := Nat.even_or_odd' N
  · -- N = 2k: S_{2k} ≤ S ≤ S_{2k+1} = S_{2k} + b(2k)
    have h1 := hb.alternating_series_le_tendsto hS k
    have h2 := hb.tendsto_le_alternating_series hS k
    rw [Finset.sum_range_succ, pow_mul, neg_one_sq, one_pow, one_mul] at h2
    rw [abs_le]
    constructor <;> linarith
  · -- N = 2k + 1: S_{2k+2} = S_{2k+1} - b(2k+1) ≤ S ≤ S_{2k+1}
    have h1 := hb.tendsto_le_alternating_series hS k
    have h2 := hb.alternating_series_le_tendsto hS (k + 1)
    rw [show 2 * (k + 1) = 2 * k + 1 + 1 by ring, Finset.sum_range_succ, pow_succ, pow_mul,
      neg_one_sq, one_pow, one_mul, neg_one_mul] at h2
    rw [abs_le]
    constructor <;> linarith

end Ch19.Enallassousa
