Convergence of Markov Chains (Perron-Frobenius)

Proof with the help of a Large Language Model (AI)

StatementLet $P$ be a column-stochastic matrix (each column has nonnegative entries summing to $1$). (i) $\lambda=1$ is always an eigenvalue of $P$ and every eigenvalue satisfies $|\lambda|\le 1$. (ii) If some power $P^{k}$ has all its entries positive, the eigenvalue $1$ is simple and there exists a unique stationary distribution $\boldsymbol{\pi}$ with $P\boldsymbol{\pi}=\boldsymbol{\pi}$, $\pi_i>0$, $\sum_i\pi_i=1$, while $P^{k}\mathbf{x}_0\to\boldsymbol{\pi}$ for every initial distribution.
