Defective (Degenerate) Eigenvalue

Proof with the help of a Large Language Model (AI)

StatementLet $\dot{\mathbf{x}}=A\mathbf{x}$ with $A$ a ($2\times2$) matrix having a double eigenvalue $\lambda$ and only one independent eigenvector $\mathbf{v}$. Then there exists a generalized eigenvector $\mathbf{w}$ with $(A-\lambda I)\mathbf{w}=\mathbf{v}$, and the general solution is $\mathbf{x}(t)=c_1e^{\lambda t}\mathbf{v}+c_2e^{\lambda t}\bigl(t\mathbf{v}+\mathbf{w}\bigr)$.
