Linear Independence of Eigenvectors

Proof with the help of a Large Language Model (AI)

StatementLet $\lambda_1,\dots,\lambda_k$ be distinct eigenvalues of $A$. (i) If $\mathbf{v}_1,\dots,\mathbf{v}_k$ are corresponding eigenvectors, then they are linearly independent. (ii) More generally, if $\mathcal{B}_i$ is a basis of the eigenspace $E_{\lambda_i}$ for each $i$, then the union $\mathcal{B}_1\cup\dots\cup\mathcal{B}_k$ is linearly independent: eigenvectors from different eigenspaces never overlap. The fact that $n$ distinct eigenvalues imply diagonalizability is a corollary of (i) and is stated as criterion (C2) in the chapter on diagonalization.
