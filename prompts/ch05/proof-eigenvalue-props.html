Properties of Eigenvalues

Proof with the help of a Large Language Model (AI)

StatementLet $A\in\mathcal{M}_{n\times n}$ with eigenvalues in $\mathbb{C}$, counted with their algebraic multiplicity. (i) $\sigma(A)=\sigma(A^{T})$: the transpose has the same eigenvalues. (ii) If $A$ is triangular, its eigenvalues are the diagonal entries. (iii) If $A\mathbf{v}=\lambda\mathbf{v}$ with $\mathbf{v}\ne\mathbf{0}$, then the same $\mathbf{v}$ is an eigenvector of $A^{k}$ (with eigenvalue $\lambda^{k}$), of $cA$ (with $c\lambda$), of $A+cI$ (with $\lambda+c$) and of $p(A)$ (with $p(\lambda)$), for every $k\in\mathbb{N}^{*}$, $c\in\mathbb{R}$ and every polynomial $p$; and if moreover $A$ is invertible, of $A^{-1}$ as well, with eigenvalue $\lambda^{-1}$. That is, the eigenvalues change, the eigendirections do not. (The relations $\operatorname{tr}(A)=\sum_i\lambda_i$ and $\det(A)=\prod_i\lambda_i$ are proved separately.)
