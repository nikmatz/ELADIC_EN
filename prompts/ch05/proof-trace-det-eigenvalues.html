1. What is proved: the statement in the book and in Lean

  • $\operatorname{tr}(A)$${}=\lambda_1+\dots+\lambda_n$
    theorem trace_sum_eigs (A : Matrix (Fin n) (Fin n) ℂ) : A.trace = A.charpoly.roots.sum
  • $\det(A)$${}=\lambda_1\lambda_2\cdots\lambda_n$
    theorem det_prod_eigs (A : Matrix (Fin n) (Fin n) ℂ) : A.det = A.charpoly.roots.prod
  • The eigenvalues in $\mathbb{C}$, counted with their multiplicity, are exactly $n$ in number
    theorem card_eigs (A : Matrix (Fin n) (Fin n) ℂ) : Multiset.card A.charpoly.roots = n
  • $A$ is invertible $\Leftrightarrow$ no eigenvalue is zero
    theorem invertible_iff_no_zero (A : Matrix (Fin n) (Fin n) ℂ) :
    IsUnit A ↔ (0 : ℂ) ∉ A.charpoly.roots

The matrix has complex entries, Matrix (Fin n) (Fin n) ℂ, and a real matrix is a special case. The eigenvalues $\lambda_1,\dots,\lambda_n$, counted with their algebraic multiplicity, are the multiset A.charpoly.roots of roots of the characteristic polynomial: each root appears as many times as its multiplicity. .sum and .prod are the sum and the product of its elements, and Multiset.card is their number. That there are $n$ roots in $\mathbb{C}$ is due to the Fundamental Theorem of Algebra: $\mathbb{C}$ is algebraically closed.

2. Where it is in Mathlib

We used the following theorems:

  • Matrix.trace_eq_sum_roots_charpoly: the trace is the sum of the roots of the characteristic polynomial.
  • Matrix.det_eq_prod_roots_charpoly: the determinant is the product of its roots.
  • IsAlgClosed.card_roots_eq_natDegree: over an algebraically closed field a polynomial has as many roots as its degree.
  • Matrix.charpoly_natDegree_eq_dim: the characteristic polynomial of an $n\times n$ matrix has degree $n$.
  • Multiset.prod_eq_zero_iff: a product is zero exactly when some factor is zero.

import Mathlib

namespace Ch5.IxnosOrizousa
open Matrix

variable {n : ℕ}

theorem trace_sum_eigs (A : Matrix (Fin n) (Fin n) ℂ) : A.trace = A.charpoly.roots.sum :=
  trace_eq_sum_roots_charpoly A

theorem det_prod_eigs (A : Matrix (Fin n) (Fin n) ℂ) : A.det = A.charpoly.roots.prod :=
  det_eq_prod_roots_charpoly A

theorem card_eigs (A : Matrix (Fin n) (Fin n) ℂ) : Multiset.card A.charpoly.roots = n := by
  rw [IsAlgClosed.card_roots_eq_natDegree, charpoly_natDegree_eq_dim, Fintype.card_fin]

theorem invertible_iff_no_zero (A : Matrix (Fin n) (Fin n) ℂ) :
    IsUnit A ↔ (0 : ℂ) ∉ A.charpoly.roots := by
  rw [isUnit_iff_isUnit_det, isUnit_iff_ne_zero, det_prod_eigs, Ne, Multiset.prod_eq_zero_iff]

end Ch5.IxnosOrizousa
