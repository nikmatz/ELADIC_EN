Properties of the Inverse Matrix

Proof with the help of a Large Language Model (AI)

StatementLet $A,B\in\mathcal{M}_{n\times n}$ be invertible and $\lambda\ne 0$: $(A^{-1})^{-1}=A$; $(AB)^{-1}=B^{-1}A^{-1}$; $(A^{T})^{-1}=(A^{-1})^{T}$; $\det(A^{-1})=\dfrac{1}{\det(A)}$; $(\lambda A)^{-1}=\dfrac{1}{\lambda}A^{-1}$; if $A$ is diagonal with $a_{ii}\ne 0$, then $A^{-1}$ is diagonal with entries $\dfrac{1}{a_{ii}}$.
