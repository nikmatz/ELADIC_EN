L'Hôpital's Rule

Proof with the help of a Large Language Model (AI)

StatementLet $f,g$ be differentiable on a neighborhood of $x_0$ (possibly except at $x_0$ itself) with $g'(x)\ne 0$. If the form $\frac{0}{0}$ ($\lim f=\lim g=0$) or $\frac{\star}{\infty}$ ($\lim|g|=+\infty$) occurs and $\lim_{x\to x_0}\dfrac{f'(x)}{g'(x)}$ exists (or is $\pm\infty$), then $\lim_{x\to x_0}\dfrac{f(x)}{g(x)}=\lim_{x\to x_0}\dfrac{f'(x)}{g'(x)}$. The rule also holds for $x_0=\pm\infty$.
