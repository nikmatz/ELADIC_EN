Criteria for Asymptotes

Proof with the help of a Large Language Model (AI)

StatementFor a rational function $f(x)=\dfrac{P(x)}{Q(x)}$ (with $P,Q$ having no common factor): (i) a vertical asymptote $x=x_0$ at every root $x_0$ of $Q$; (ii) a horizontal asymptote if $\deg P\le\deg Q$ — namely $y=0$ when $\deg P<\deg Q$, or $y$ equal to the ratio of the leading coefficients when $\deg P=\deg Q$; (iii) an oblique asymptote $y=\alpha x+\beta$ if and only if $\deg P=\deg Q+1$, where $\alpha x+\beta$ is the quotient of the division $P/Q$. More generally (not only for rational functions), $y=\alpha x+\beta$ is an oblique asymptote of $f$ at $\pm\infty$ if and only if the finite limits $\alpha=\lim_{x\to\pm\infty}\dfrac{f(x)}{x}\ne 0$ and $\beta=\lim_{x\to\pm\infty}\bigl(f(x)-\alpha x\bigr)$ exist. If $\alpha=0$ and $\beta$ exists, the asymptote is horizontal.
