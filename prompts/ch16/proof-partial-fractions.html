Integration by Partial Fraction Decomposition

Proof with the help of a Large Language Model (AI)

StatementEvery proper rational function $\dfrac{P(x)}{Q(x)}$ (with $\deg P<\deg Q$) decomposes uniquely into a sum of simple fractions: to every linear factor $(x-a)^k$ of $Q$ correspond the terms $\dfrac{A_1}{x-a}+\cdots+\dfrac{A_k}{(x-a)^k}$; to every irreducible quadratic factor $(x^2+px+q)^m$ (with $p^2-4q<0$) correspond the terms $\dfrac{B_1x+\Gamma_1}{x^2+px+q}+\cdots+\dfrac{B_mx+\Gamma_m}{(x^2+px+q)^m}$. Integration then yields logarithms and powers (linear terms) or logarithms and arctangents (irreducible quadratic terms).
