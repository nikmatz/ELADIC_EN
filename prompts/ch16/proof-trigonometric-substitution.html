Trigonometric Substitution

Proof with the help of a Large Language Model (AI)

StatementLet $a\gt 0$. For radicals (with the restrictions on the angle that keep the root nonnegative): $\sqrt{a^2-x^2}\to x=a\sin\theta$, $\theta\in[-\tfrac{\pi}{2},\tfrac{\pi}{2}]$ ($1-\sin^2\theta=\cos^2\theta$); $\sqrt{a^2+x^2}\to x=a\tan\theta$, $\theta\in(-\tfrac{\pi}{2},\tfrac{\pi}{2})$ ($1+\tan^2\theta=\sec^2\theta$); $\sqrt{x^2-a^2}\to x=a\sec\theta$, $\theta\in[0,\tfrac{\pi}{2})\cup(\tfrac{\pi}{2},\pi]$ ($\sec^2\theta-1=\tan^2\theta$; then $\sqrt{x^2-a^2}=a|\tan\theta|$, that is, $a\tan\theta$ for $\theta\in[0,\tfrac{\pi}{2})$ when $x\ge a$, and $-a\tan\theta$ for $\theta\in(\tfrac{\pi}{2},\pi]$ when $x\le -a$).
