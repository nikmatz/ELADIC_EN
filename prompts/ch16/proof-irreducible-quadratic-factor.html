Irreducible Quadratic Factor (Completing the Square)

Proof with the help of a Large Language Model (AI)

StatementLet $x^2+bx+c$ be irreducible ($b^2-4c<0$). Completing the square gives $x^2+bx+c=\left(x+\tfrac{b}{2}\right)^2+k^2$, with $k=\sqrt{c-\tfrac{b^2}{4}}>0$, and $\int\dfrac{Ax+B}{x^2+bx+c}dx$ splits into a logarithmic part (whose numerator is the derivative $2x+b$) and an arctangent.
