Cauchy–Hadamard Formula (Radius of Convergence)

Proof with the help of a Large Language Model (AI)

StatementFor the power series $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} c_n (x-x_0)^n$, the radius of convergence is given by the Cauchy–Hadamard formula $R=\dfrac{1}{\limsup_{n\to\infty}\sqrt[n]{|c_n|}}$, with the conventions $\dfrac{1}{0}=+\infty$ and $\dfrac{1}{+\infty}=0$. The series converges absolutely for $|x-x_0|\lt R$ and diverges for $|x-x_0|\gt R$ (the endpoints require separate examination). Practical form: if $c_n\ne 0$ for every sufficiently large $n$ and the limit $\lim_{n\to\infty}\left|\dfrac{c_n}{c_{n+1}}\right|$ exists, then it equals $R$.
