Taylor and Maclaurin Series

Proof with the help of a Large Language Model (AI)

StatementLet $f$ have derivatives of every order on an open interval containing $x_0$. The equality $f(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\dfrac{f^{(n)}(x_0)}{n!}(x-x_0)^n$ holds if and only if $\lim_{n\to\infty}R_n(x)=0$, where $R_n(x)=\dfrac{f^{(n+1)}(\xi)}{(n+1)!}(x-x_0)^{n+1}$ (the Lagrange remainder, with $\xi$ between $x_0$ and $x$). For $x_0=0$ we obtain the Maclaurin series.
