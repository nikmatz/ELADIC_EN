Differentiation and Integration of Power Series

StatementLet $f(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}c_n(x-x_0)^n$ with radius of convergence $R>0$. Then for $|x-x_0|<R$ the function $f$ is differentiable and $f′(x)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n\,c_n(x-x_0)^{n-1}$, $\int f(x)\,dx=C+\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\dfrac{c_n}{n+1}(x-x_0)^{n+1}$, with the same radius of convergence $R$.
