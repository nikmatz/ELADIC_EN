1. What is proved: the statement in the book and in Lean

  • If $p,q$ are continuous on $[c,d]$ and $q\le p$, the area of the region between $x=q(y)$, $x=p(y)$ and the lines $y=c$, $y=d$ is $E$${}=\int_c^d\bigl[p(y)-q(y)\bigr]\,dy$
    theorem area_dy (p q : ℝ → ℝ) (c d : ℝ) (hcd : c ≤ d)
    (hp : ContinuousOn p (Set.Icc c d)) (hq : ContinuousOn q (Set.Icc c d))
    (hqp : ∀ y ∈ Set.Icc c d, q y ≤ p y) :
    volume {z : ℝ × ℝ | z.2 ∈ Set.Icc c d ∧ z.1 ∈ Set.Ioo (q z.2) (p z.2)} =
      ENNReal.ofReal (∫ y in c..d, (p y - q y))

In Mathlib, the area of a plane region is its Lebesgue measure, volume. The area is a nonnegative number or $+\infty$ (type ENNReal); that is why the integral is written inside ENNReal.ofReal, which converts it into such a number. The region is the set of the points $(x,y)$ with $y\in[c,d]$ and $q(y)\lt x\lt p(y)$. The proof reduces to integration with respect to $x$: swapping the axes, $(x,y)\mapsto(y,x)$, preserves area and turns the region into the region between the graphs of $q$ and $p$.

2. Where it is in Mathlib

We used the following theorems:

  • MeasureTheory.Measure.measurePreserving_swap: the swap $(x,y)\mapsto(y,x)$ preserves area.
  • MeasurableEquiv.prodComm: the swap $(x,y)\mapsto(y,x)$, as an invertible map.
  • MeasureTheory.MeasurePreserving.measure_preimage_equiv: such a map does not change the area of sets.
  • volume_regionBetween_eq_integral: the area of the region between two graphs.
  • MeasureTheory.Measure.volume_eq_prod: area in the plane is the product of the length measures on the two axes.
  • ContinuousOn.integrableOn_Icc: a function continuous on a closed interval is integrable there.
  • intervalIntegral.integral_of_le: for $a\le b$, $\int_a^b$ is an integral over $(a,b]$.
  • MeasureTheory.integral_Icc_eq_integral_Ioc: the integrals over $[a,b]$ and $(a,b]$ are equal.

open MeasureTheory

-- the region: the points (x, y) with y ∈ [c, d] and q(y) < x < p(y)
theorem area_dy (p q : ℝ → ℝ) (c d : ℝ) (hcd : c ≤ d)
    (hp : ContinuousOn p (Set.Icc c d)) (hq : ContinuousOn q (Set.Icc c d))
    (hqp : ∀ y ∈ Set.Icc c d, q y ≤ p y) :
    volume {z : ℝ × ℝ | z.2 ∈ Set.Icc c d ∧ z.1 ∈ Set.Ioo (q z.2) (p z.2)} =
      ENNReal.ofReal (∫ y in c..d, (p y - q y)) := by
  -- swapping the axes (x, y) ↦ (y, x) preserves area
  have hswap : MeasurePreserving (MeasurableEquiv.prodComm : ℝ × ℝ ≃ᵐ ℝ × ℝ)
      (volume.prod volume) (volume.prod volume) := Measure.measurePreserving_swap
  -- after the swap, the region is the region between the graphs of q and p
  have hset : {z : ℝ × ℝ | z.2 ∈ Set.Icc c d ∧ z.1 ∈ Set.Ioo (q z.2) (p z.2)} =
      (MeasurableEquiv.prodComm : ℝ × ℝ ≃ᵐ ℝ × ℝ) ⁻¹' regionBetween q p (Set.Icc c d) := rfl
  rw [Measure.volume_eq_prod, hset, hswap.measure_preimage_equiv,
    volume_regionBetween_eq_integral hq.integrableOn_Icc hp.integrableOn_Icc measurableSet_Icc hqp,
    intervalIntegral.integral_of_le hcd, integral_Icc_eq_integral_Ioc]
  rfl

end Ch15.EmvadonWsProsY
