Symmetric Matrices: Always Orthogonally Diagonalizable

Proof with the help of a Large Language Model (AI)

StatementThe key step of the Spectral Theorem: in a real symmetric matrix $A=A^{T}$ no eigenvalue is defective, that is, $m_g(\lambda)=m_a(\lambda)$ for every eigenvalue $\lambda$. From this, together with the orthogonality of eigenvectors of different eigenvalues, the orthogonal diagonalization $A=Q\Lambda Q^{T}$ follows. Caution: the claim $m_g=m_a$ may not be used as a step in a proof of diagonalizability itself, since it is precisely what is to be shown; the proof goes through the complex inner-product structure or through induction on the dimension.
