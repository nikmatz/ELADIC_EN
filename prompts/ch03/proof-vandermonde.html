The Vandermonde Matrix and Interpolation

StatementFor $n+1$ points $(x_0,y_0),\dots,(x_n,y_n)$ with pairwise distinct abscissas ($x_i\ne x_j$ for $i\ne j$), the unique polynomial $p(x)=a_0+a_1 x+\dots+a_n x^n$ of degree $\le n$ that interpolates them satisfies $V a=y$ with $V_{ij}=x_i^{\,j}$ (the Vandermonde matrix). Since the $x_i$ are distinct, $\det(V)\ne 0$ and the solution is unique.
