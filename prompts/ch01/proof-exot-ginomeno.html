Properties of the Cross Product

Chapter 1 · Explanation by a Large Language Model and a proof checked by computer (Lean)

StatementIn $\mathbb{R}^3$ the cross (vector) product satisfies: anticommutativity $u\times v=-(v\times u)$, linearity, $u\times v$ is perpendicular to both $u$ and $v$, $\|u\times v\|=\|u\|\,\|v\|\sin\theta$, and $u\times v=\mathbf{0}\Leftrightarrow$ $u,v$ are linearly dependent (parallel).

1. What is proved: the statement in the book and in Lean

  • Anticommutativity: $u\times v=-(v\times u)$
    theorem anticomm (u v : Fin 3 → ℝ) : u ⨯₃ v = -(v ⨯₃ u)
  • Linearity: $(u+w)\times v=u\times v+w\times v$
    theorem add_left (u w v : Fin 3 → ℝ) : (u + w) ⨯₃ v = u ⨯₃ v + w ⨯₃ v
  • Linearity: $(cu)\times v=c\,(u\times v)$
    theorem smul_left (c : ℝ) (u v : Fin 3 → ℝ) : (c • u) ⨯₃ v = c • (u ⨯₃ v)
  • $u\times v$ is perpendicular to $u$
    theorem perp_left (u v : Fin 3 → ℝ) : u ⬝ᵥ (u ⨯₃ v) = 0
  • $u\times v$ is perpendicular to $v$
    theorem perp_right (u v : Fin 3 → ℝ) : v ⬝ᵥ (u ⨯₃ v) = 0
  • $\|u\times v\|$${}=\|u\|\,\|v\|\sin\theta$, in the form $\|u\times v\|^2$${}=\|u\|^2\|v\|^2-(u\cdot v)^2$
    theorem lagrange (u v : Fin 3 → ℝ) :
    (u ⨯₃ v) ⬝ᵥ (u ⨯₃ v) = (u ⬝ᵥ u) * (v ⬝ᵥ v) - (u ⬝ᵥ v) ^ 2
  • $u\times v$${}=\mathbf{0}\Leftrightarrow$ $u,v$ are linearly dependent
    theorem zero_iff (u v : Fin 3 → ℝ) : u ⨯₃ v = 0 ↔ ¬ LinearIndependent ℝ ![u, v]

The vectors of $\mathbb{R}^3$ are the triples of numbers Fin 3 → ℝ. $u\times v$ is written u ⨯₃ v and $u\cdot v$ is written u ⬝ᵥ v, so $\|x\|^2$ is x ⬝ᵥ x. The formula with the sine is written without an angle: since $u\cdot v=\|u\|\,\|v\|\cos\theta$, Lagrange’s identity gives $\|u\times v\|^2$${}=\|u\|^2\|v\|^2(1-\cos^2\theta)$${}=\|u\|^2\|v\|^2\sin^2\theta$.

2. Step by step: $u\times v$ is perpendicular to $u$

In the book this follows by computing with coordinates: $u\cdot(u\times v)=0$. We do the same in Lean. We start from the goal. Here Lean displays $u\times v$ as (crossProduct u) v:

The goal
u v : Fin 3 → ℝ
⊢ u ⬝ᵥ (crossProduct u) v = 0

Above ⊢ is what we know; after ⊢ is what we have to show. After each step we show only what changed.

  1. rw [cross_apply]

    We write $u\times v$ in coordinates (theorem cross_apply): its three coordinates are $u_2v_3-u_3v_2$, $u_3v_1-u_1v_3$ and $u_1v_2-u_2v_1$. Lean numbers the coordinates from $0$, so u 0 is $u_1$.

    What remains to be proved
    ⊢ u ⬝ᵥ ![u 1 * v 2 - u 2 * v 1, u 2 * v 0 - u 0 * v 2, u 0 * v 1 - u 1 * v 0] = 0
  2. rw [vec3_dotProduct]

    We expand the dot product in $\mathbb{R}^3$: $x\cdot y=x_1y_1+x_2y_2+x_3y_3$ (theorem vec3_dotProduct).

    What remains to be proved
    ⊢ u 0 * ![u 1 * v 2 - u 2 * v 1, u 2 * v 0 - u 0 * v 2, u 0 * v 1 - u 1 * v 0] 0 +
      u 1 * ![u 1 * v 2 - u 2 * v 1, u 2 * v 0 - u 0 * v 2, u 0 * v 1 - u 1 * v 0] 1 +
    u 2 * ![u 1 * v 2 - u 2 * v 1, u 2 * v 0 - u 0 * v 2, u 0 * v 1 - u 1 * v 0] 2 =
  0
  3. dsimp only [Matrix.cons_val]

    We read off the coordinates of ![a, b, c]: position 0 is a, position 1 is b and position 2 is c.

    What remains to be proved
    ⊢ u 0 * (u 1 * v 2 - u 2 * v 1) + u 1 * (u 2 * v 0 - u 0 * v 2) + u 2 * (u 0 * v 1 - u 1 * v 0) = 0
  4. ring

    What remains is an algebraic identity: if we carry out the operations, the terms cancel in pairs. ring checks it.

    ✓ No goals: the proof is complete.

import Mathlib

namespace Ch1.ExotGinomeno
open Matrix

theorem anticomm (u v : Fin 3 → ℝ) : u ⨯₃ v = -(v ⨯₃ u) := by
  rw [cross_anticomm]

theorem add_left (u w v : Fin 3 → ℝ) : (u + w) ⨯₃ v = u ⨯₃ v + w ⨯₃ v := by
  rw [map_add, LinearMap.add_apply]

theorem smul_left (c : ℝ) (u v : Fin 3 → ℝ) : (c • u) ⨯₃ v = c • (u ⨯₃ v) := by
  rw [map_smul, LinearMap.smul_apply]

theorem perp_left (u v : Fin 3 → ℝ) : u ⬝ᵥ (u ⨯₃ v) = 0 := by
  rw [cross_apply]
  rw [vec3_dotProduct]
  dsimp only [Matrix.cons_val]
  ring

theorem perp_right (u v : Fin 3 → ℝ) : v ⬝ᵥ (u ⨯₃ v) = 0 := by
  rw [cross_apply, vec3_dotProduct]
  dsimp only [Matrix.cons_val]
  ring

theorem lagrange (u v : Fin 3 → ℝ) :
    (u ⨯₃ v) ⬝ᵥ (u ⨯₃ v) = (u ⬝ᵥ u) * (v ⬝ᵥ v) - (u ⬝ᵥ v) ^ 2 := by
  rw [cross_dot_cross, dotProduct_comm v u]
  ring

theorem zero_iff (u v : Fin 3 → ℝ) : u ⨯₃ v = 0 ↔ ¬ LinearIndependent ℝ ![u, v] := by
  rw [← crossProduct_ne_zero_iff_linearIndependent, not_not]

end Ch1.ExotGinomeno
