1. What is proved: the statement in the book and in Lean

  • $|\langle u,v\rangle|\le\|u\|\,\|v\|$
    theorem cauchy_schwarz (u v : V) : |⟪u, v⟫_ℝ| ≤ ‖u‖ * ‖v‖
  • Equivalently, squared: $\langle u,v\rangle^2\le\|u\|^2\|v\|^2$
    theorem sq_version (u v : V) : ⟪u, v⟫_ℝ ^ 2 ≤ ‖u‖ ^ 2 * ‖v‖ ^ 2
  • Equality $\Leftrightarrow$ $u,v$ are linearly dependent
    theorem equality_iff (u v : V) :
    |⟪u, v⟫_ℝ| = ‖u‖ * ‖v‖ ↔ ¬ LinearIndependent ℝ ![u, v]

The inner product $\langle u,v\rangle$ is written ⟪u, v⟫_ℝ and the norm $\|u\|$ is written ‖u‖. V is any real inner product space (InnerProductSpace ℝ V), so the result holds in $\mathbb{R}^n$ as well. t • v is the product $tv$ of the number $t$ with the vector $v$. ¬ LinearIndependent ℝ ![u, v] means that $u,v$ are not linearly independent, that is, they are linearly dependent.

2. Step by step: the inequality $\langle u,v\rangle^2\le\|u\|^2\|v\|^2$

The proof in the book: for every $t\in\mathbb{R}$ we have $\|u+tv\|^2\ge 0$, so the quadratic in $t$ has discriminant $\le 0$. We first prove the squared inequality. The form with the absolute value follows immediately and is the exercise below. We start from the goal:

The goal
V : Type u_1
inst✝¹ : NormedAddCommGroup V
inst✝ : InnerProductSpace ℝ V
u v : V
⊢ ⟪u, v⟫_ℝ ^ 2 ≤ ‖u‖ ^ 2 * ‖v‖ ^ 2

Above ⊢ is what we know; after ⊢ is what we have to show. After each step we show only what changed.

    We open an intermediate claim h: the quadratic $\|v\|^2t^2+2\langle u,v\rangle t+\|u\|^2$ takes no negative values. have … := by opens a proof of its own, with its own goal.

    What remains to be proved
    ⊢ ∀ (t : ℝ), 0 ≤ ‖v‖ ^ 2 * (t * t) + 2 * ⟪u, v⟫_ℝ * t + ‖u‖ ^ 2
  2. intro t

    We take an arbitrary real number $t$.

    What remains to be proved
    t : ℝ
⊢ 0 ≤ ‖v‖ ^ 2 * (t * t) + 2 * ⟪u, v⟫_ℝ * t + ‖u‖ ^ 2

    The lemma expand, a little earlier in the code, expands the square: $\|u+tv\|^2$${}=\|u\|^2+2t\langle u,v\rangle+t^2\|v\|^2$.

    What remains to be proved
    e : ‖u + t • v‖ ^ 2 = ‖u‖ ^ 2 + 2 * (t * ⟪u, v⟫_ℝ) + t ^ 2 * ‖v‖ ^ 2
⊢ 0 ≤ ‖v‖ ^ 2 * (t * t) + 2 * ⟪u, v⟫_ℝ * t + ‖u‖ ^ 2

    A square is never negative: $\|u+tv\|^2\ge 0$.

    What remains to be proved
    p : 0 ≤ ‖u + t • v‖ ^ 2
⊢ 0 ≤ ‖v‖ ^ 2 * (t * t) + 2 * ⟪u, v⟫_ℝ * t + ‖u‖ ^ 2

    The goal follows from e and p: it is the same inequality with the terms in a different order. The tactic nlinarith does the combination. The intermediate claim h is proved.

    ✓ This part is complete.
  6. have hd := discrim_le_zero h

    A quadratic $at^2+bt+c$ that never takes a negative value has discriminant $\le 0$. This is the Mathlib theorem discrim_le_zero. hd says that $\Delta\le 0$.

    We unfold the definition of the discriminant: $\Delta$${}=b^2-4ac$${}=(2\langle u,v\rangle)^2-4\|v\|^2\|u\|^2$.

Hint

The theorem abs_le_of_sq_le_sq says: if $a^2\le b^2$ and $b\ge 0$, then $|a|\le b$. Start with apply abs_le_of_sq_le_sq _ (by positivity). Rewrite $(\|u\|\,\|v\|)^2=\|u\|^2\|v\|^2$ with rw [mul_pow] and finish with exact sq_version u v.

Solution
theorem cauchy_schwarz (u v : V) : |⟪u, v⟫_ℝ| ≤ ‖u‖ * ‖v‖ := by
  apply abs_le_of_sq_le_sq _ (by positivity)
  rw [mul_pow]
  exact sq_version u v

-- Chapter 1 · Cauchy–Schwarz Inequality · checked with Lean v4.37.0-rc1 and Mathlib (22/11/2026).

import Mathlib

namespace Ch1.CauchySchwarz
open scoped InnerProductSpace

variable {V : Type*} [NormedAddCommGroup V] [InnerProductSpace ℝ V]

theorem expand (u v : V) (t : ℝ) :
    ‖u + t • v‖ ^ 2 = ‖u‖ ^ 2 + 2 * (t * ⟪u, v⟫_ℝ) + t ^ 2 * ‖v‖ ^ 2 := by
  rw [norm_add_sq_real, real_inner_smul_right, norm_smul, mul_pow, Real.norm_eq_abs, sq_abs]

theorem sq_version (u v : V) : ⟪u, v⟫_ℝ ^ 2 ≤ ‖u‖ ^ 2 * ‖v‖ ^ 2 := by
  have h : ∀ t : ℝ, 0 ≤ ‖v‖ ^ 2 * (t * t) + 2 * ⟪u, v⟫_ℝ * t + ‖u‖ ^ 2 := by
    intro t
    have e := expand u v t
    have p := sq_nonneg ‖u + t • v‖
    nlinarith [e, p]
  have hd := discrim_le_zero h
  unfold discrim at hd
  nlinarith [hd]

theorem cauchy_schwarz (u v : V) : |⟪u, v⟫_ℝ| ≤ ‖u‖ * ‖v‖ := by
  apply abs_le_of_sq_le_sq _ (by positivity)
  rw [mul_pow]
  exact sq_version u v

theorem equality_iff (u v : V) :
    |⟪u, v⟫_ℝ| = ‖u‖ * ‖v‖ ↔ ¬ LinearIndependent ℝ ![u, v] := by
  by_cases hu : u = 0
  · subst hu
    simp only [inner_zero_left, abs_zero, norm_zero, zero_mul, true_iff]
    intro h
    exact h.ne_zero 0 rfl
  · have key := (norm_inner_eq_norm_tfae ℝ u v).out 1 3
    rw [← Real.norm_eq_abs, key, LinearIndependent.pair_iff' hu]
    simp only [hu, false_or, not_forall, not_not, ne_eq]
    constructor
    · rintro ⟨r, hr⟩
      exact ⟨r, hr.symm⟩
    · rintro ⟨r, hr⟩
      exact ⟨r, hr.symm⟩

end Ch1.CauchySchwarz
