1. What is proved: the statement in the book and in Lean

  • Three vectors of $\mathbb{R}^3$, the columns of a $3\times 3$ matrix $A$, are linearly independent $\Leftrightarrow\det A\neq 0$
    theorem independent_iff_det (A : Matrix (Fin 3) (Fin 3) ℝ) :
    LinearIndependent ℝ A.col ↔ A.det ≠ 0

The matrix is A : Matrix (Fin 3) (Fin 3) ℝ and its columns are the family A.col. The determinant is written A.det. Mathlib proves this for square matrices of every size, through the notion of a nonsingular matrix (Nonsingular).

2. Where it is in Mathlib

We used the following theorems:

  • Matrix.linearIndependent_col_iff: the columns are independent exactly when the matrix is nonsingular.
  • Matrix.nonsingular_iff_det_ne_zero: nonsingular matrix $\Leftrightarrow$ determinant $\neq 0$.
