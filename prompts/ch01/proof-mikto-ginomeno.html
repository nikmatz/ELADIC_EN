Properties of the Scalar Triple Product

StatementIn $\mathbb{R}^3$ the scalar triple product $(u\times v)\cdot w$ equals the $3\times 3$ determinant whose rows are $u,v,w$, it is invariant under cyclic permutation and changes sign when two vectors are interchanged (antisymmetry); its absolute value gives the volume of the parallelepiped, and $(u\times v)\cdot w=0\Leftrightarrow$ $u,v,w$ are coplanar (linearly dependent).

1. What is proved: the statement in the book and in Lean

  • $(u\times v)\cdot w$ equals the determinant with rows $u,v,w$
    theorem eq_det (u v w : Fin 3 → ℝ) : (u ⨯₃ v) ⬝ᵥ w = Matrix.det ![u, v, w]
  • Cyclic permutation: $(u\times v)\cdot w$${}=(v\times w)\cdot u$
    theorem cyclic (u v w : Fin 3 → ℝ) : (u ⨯₃ v) ⬝ᵥ w = (v ⨯₃ w) ⬝ᵥ u
  • Interchanging two vectors changes the sign: $(v\times u)\cdot w$${}=-(u\times v)\cdot w$
    theorem swap (u v w : Fin 3 → ℝ) : (v ⨯₃ u) ⬝ᵥ w = -((u ⨯₃ v) ⬝ᵥ w)
  • $(u\times v)\cdot w$${}=0\Leftrightarrow$ $u,v,w$ are linearly dependent
    theorem zero_iff (u v w : Fin 3 → ℝ) :
    (u ⨯₃ v) ⬝ᵥ w = 0 ↔ ¬ LinearIndependent ℝ ![u, v, w]

The product $(u\times v)\cdot w$ is written (u ⨯₃ v) ⬝ᵥ w, and the determinant with rows $u,v,w$ is written Matrix.det ![u, v, w]. The volume of the parallelepiped is available in Mathlib through measure theory, and we do not state it here.

2. Where it is in Mathlib

We used the following theorems:

  • triple_product_eq_det: the scalar triple product as a determinant.
  • triple_product_permutation: cyclic permutation.
  • cross_anticomm: antisymmetry of the cross product.
  • Matrix.linearIndependent_row_iff: the rows are independent exactly when the matrix is nonsingular.
  • Matrix.nonsingular_iff_det_ne_zero: nonsingular matrix $\Leftrightarrow$ determinant $\neq 0$.

import Mathlib

namespace Ch1.MiktoGinomeno
open Matrix

theorem eq_det (u v w : Fin 3 → ℝ) : (u ⨯₃ v) ⬝ᵥ w = Matrix.det ![u, v, w] := by
  rw [dotProduct_comm, triple_product_permutation, triple_product_eq_det]

theorem cyclic (u v w : Fin 3 → ℝ) : (u ⨯₃ v) ⬝ᵥ w = (v ⨯₃ w) ⬝ᵥ u := by
  rw [dotProduct_comm, triple_product_permutation, dotProduct_comm]

theorem swap (u v w : Fin 3 → ℝ) : (v ⨯₃ u) ⬝ᵥ w = -((u ⨯₃ v) ⬝ᵥ w) := by
  rw [← cross_anticomm, neg_dotProduct]

theorem independent_iff_det (a b c : Fin 3 → ℝ) :
    LinearIndependent ℝ ![a, b, c] ↔ Matrix.det ![a, b, c] ≠ 0 :=
  (Matrix.linearIndependent_row_iff (A := Matrix.of ![a, b, c])).trans
    Matrix.nonsingular_iff_det_ne_zero

theorem zero_iff (u v w : Fin 3 → ℝ) :
    (u ⨯₃ v) ⬝ᵥ w = 0 ↔ ¬ LinearIndependent ℝ ![u, v, w] := by
  rw [eq_det, independent_iff_det, not_not]

end Ch1.MiktoGinomeno
