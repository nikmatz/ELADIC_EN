Orthogonal Projection Theorem

Chapter 1 · Explanation by a Large Language Model and a proof checked by computer (Lean)

StatementLet $W$ be a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^n$ and $\mathbf{u}\in\mathbb{R}^n$. Then: (i) $\mathbf{u}$ is written uniquely as $\mathbf{u}=\mathbf{p}+\mathbf{e}$ with $\mathbf{p}\in W$ and $\mathbf{e}\in W^{\perp}$, where $\mathbf{p}=\mathrm{proj}_W(\mathbf{u})$; (ii) $\mathbf{p}$ is the point of $W$ closest to $\mathbf{u}$: $\|\mathbf{u}-\mathbf{p}\|<\|\mathbf{u}-\mathbf{w}\|$ for every $\mathbf{w}\in W$ with $\mathbf{w}\neq\mathbf{p}$. The condition $\mathbf{e}\perp W$ gives the normal equations $A^{T}A\,\hat{\mathbf{x}}=A^{T}\mathbf{u}$ (the columns of $A$ forming a basis of $W$); the projection matrix is $P=A(A^{T}A)^{-1}A^{T}$, with $P^{2}=P$ and $P^{T}=P$.

1. What is proved: the statement in the book and in Lean

  • The projection $\mathbf{p}$${}=\mathrm{proj}_W(\mathbf{u})$ belongs to $W$ and $\mathbf{e}$${}=\mathbf{u}-\mathbf{p}\in W^{\perp}$
    theorem decomposition (W : Submodule ℝ (EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin n))) (u : EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin n)) :
    W.starProjection u ∈ W ∧ u - W.starProjection u ∈ Wᗮ
  • Uniqueness: if $\mathbf{u}=\mathbf{p}+\mathbf{e}$ with $\mathbf{p}\in W$ and $\mathbf{e}\in W^{\perp}$, then $\mathbf{p}$${}=\mathrm{proj}_W(\mathbf{u})$
    theorem unique (W : Submodule ℝ (EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin n))) (u p e : EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin n))
    (hp : p ∈ W) (he : e ∈ Wᗮ) (h : u = p + e) : W.starProjection u = p
  • Minimum distance: $\|\mathbf{u}-\mathbf{p}\|\le\|\mathbf{u}-\mathbf{w}\|$ for every $\mathbf{w}\in W$
    theorem closest (W : Submodule ℝ (EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin n))) (u w : EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin n))
    (hw : w ∈ W) : ‖u - W.starProjection u‖ ≤ ‖u - w‖
  • And strictly: $\|\mathbf{u}-\mathbf{p}\|\lt\|\mathbf{u}-\mathbf{w}\|$ for $\mathbf{w}\in W$ with $\mathbf{w}\neq\mathbf{p}$
    theorem closest_strict (W : Submodule ℝ (EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin n))) (u w : EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin n))
    (hw : w ∈ W) (hne : w ≠ W.starProjection u) : ‖u - W.starProjection u‖ < ‖u - w‖

$\mathbb{R}^n$ is EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin n), with the usual inner product. The orthogonal projection onto $W$ is written W.starProjection, and $W^{\perp}$ is written Wᗮ. For the minimum distance we use the Pythagorean theorem: since $\mathbf{u}-\mathbf{p}\perp\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{w}$, we have $\|\mathbf{u}-\mathbf{w}\|^2$${}=\|\mathbf{u}-\mathbf{p}\|^2+\|\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{w}\|^2$. The normal equations and the projection matrix $P$ are not stated here.

2. Where it is in Mathlib

We used the following theorems:

  • Submodule.starProjection: the orthogonal projection onto a subspace.
  • Submodule.starProjection_apply_mem: $\mathrm{proj}_W(\mathbf{u})\in W$.
  • Submodule.sub_starProjection_mem_orthogonal: $\mathbf{u}-\mathrm{proj}_W(\mathbf{u})\in W^{\perp}$.
  • Submodule.eq_starProjection_of_mem_of_inner_eq_zero: the uniqueness of the projection.
  • norm_add_sq_eq_norm_sq_add_norm_sq_real: the Pythagorean theorem.

import Mathlib

namespace Ch1.OrthogonalProjection
open scoped InnerProductSpace
open Submodule

variable {n : ℕ}

theorem decomposition (W : Submodule ℝ (EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin n))) (u : EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin n)) :
    W.starProjection u ∈ W ∧ u - W.starProjection u ∈ Wᗮ :=
  ⟨W.starProjection_apply_mem u, W.sub_starProjection_mem_orthogonal u⟩

theorem unique (W : Submodule ℝ (EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin n))) (u p e : EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin n))
    (hp : p ∈ W) (he : e ∈ Wᗮ) (h : u = p + e) : W.starProjection u = p := by
  apply eq_starProjection_of_mem_of_inner_eq_zero hp
  intro w hw
  rw [h, add_sub_cancel_left]
  exact inner_left_of_mem_orthogonal hw he

theorem closest (W : Submodule ℝ (EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin n))) (u w : EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin n))
    (hw : w ∈ W) : ‖u - W.starProjection u‖ ≤ ‖u - w‖ := by
  set p := W.starProjection u
  have hpw : p - w ∈ W := W.sub_mem (W.starProjection_apply_mem u) hw
  have h0 : ⟪u - p, p - w⟫_ℝ = 0 :=
    inner_left_of_mem_orthogonal hpw (W.sub_starProjection_mem_orthogonal u)
  have pyth := norm_add_sq_eq_norm_sq_add_norm_sq_real h0
  rw [sub_add_sub_cancel] at pyth
  have hsq : ‖u - p‖ ^ 2 ≤ ‖u - w‖ ^ 2 := by nlinarith [mul_self_nonneg ‖p - w‖]
  exact le_of_sq_le_sq hsq (norm_nonneg _)

theorem closest_strict (W : Submodule ℝ (EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin n))) (u w : EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin n))
    (hw : w ∈ W) (hne : w ≠ W.starProjection u) : ‖u - W.starProjection u‖ < ‖u - w‖ := by
  set p := W.starProjection u
  have hpw : p - w ∈ W := W.sub_mem (W.starProjection_apply_mem u) hw
  have h0 : ⟪u - p, p - w⟫_ℝ = 0 :=
    inner_left_of_mem_orthogonal hpw (W.sub_starProjection_mem_orthogonal u)
  have pyth := norm_add_sq_eq_norm_sq_add_norm_sq_real h0
  rw [sub_add_sub_cancel] at pyth
  have hpos : 0 < ‖p - w‖ := norm_pos_iff.mpr (sub_ne_zero.mpr (Ne.symm hne))
  have hsq : ‖u - p‖ ^ 2 < ‖u - w‖ ^ 2 := by nlinarith [mul_pos hpos hpos]
  exact lt_of_pow_lt_pow_left₀ 2 (norm_nonneg _) hsq

end Ch1.OrthogonalProjection
