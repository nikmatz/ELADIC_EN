Orthogonal Projection Theorem

Proof with the help of a Large Language Model (AI)

StatementLet $W$ be a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^n$ and $\mathbf{u}\in\mathbb{R}^n$. Then: (i) $\mathbf{u}$ is written uniquely as $\mathbf{u}=\mathbf{p}+\mathbf{e}$ with $\mathbf{p}\in W$ and $\mathbf{e}\in W^{\perp}$, where $\mathbf{p}=\mathrm{proj}_W(\mathbf{u})$; (ii) $\mathbf{p}$ is the point of $W$ closest to $\mathbf{u}$: $\|\mathbf{u}-\mathbf{p}\|<\|\mathbf{u}-\mathbf{w}\|$ for every $\mathbf{w}\in W$ with $\mathbf{w}\neq\mathbf{p}$. The condition $\mathbf{e}\perp W$ gives the normal equations $A^{T}A\,\hat{\mathbf{x}}=A^{T}\mathbf{u}$ (the columns of $A$ forming a basis of $W$); the projection matrix is $P=A(A^{T}A)^{-1}A^{T}$, with $P^{2}=P$ and $P^{T}=P$.
