QR Factorization

Proof with the help of a Large Language Model (AI)

StatementLet $A$ be an $m\times n$ matrix with linearly independent columns $\mathbf{a}_1,\dots,\mathbf{a}_n$. Then $A$ can be written as $A=QR$, where $Q$ ($m\times n$) has as its columns the orthonormal basis $\mathbf{q}_1,\dots,\mathbf{q}_n$ produced by Gram–Schmidt (so $Q^{T}Q=I_n$) and $R$ ($n\times n$) is upper triangular with positive diagonal: $r_{ij}=\langle \mathbf{a}_j,\mathbf{q}_i\rangle$ for $i<j$ and $r_{jj}=\bigl\|\mathbf{a}_j-\sum_{i<j} r_{ij}\mathbf{q}_i\bigr\|>0$. Equivalently, $R=Q^{T}A$.
