The Gamma Function (Euler)

Proof with the help of a Large Language Model (AI)

Statement$\Gamma(x)=\int_0^{+\infty}e^{-t}t^{x-1}\,dt$, $x\gt 0$. Properties: $\Gamma(x+1)=x\Gamma(x)$, $\Gamma(n+1)=n!$ for $n\in\mathbb{N}$, $\Gamma\!\left(\tfrac{1}{2}\right)=\sqrt{\pi}$. Also, for $s\gt 0$ and $n\in\mathbb{N}_0$ we have $\int_0^{+\infty}t^n e^{-st}\,dt=\dfrac{n!}{s^{n+1}}=\dfrac{\Gamma(n+1)}{s^{n+1}}$ (for $s\le 0$ the integral diverges).
