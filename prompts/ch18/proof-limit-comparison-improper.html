The Limit Comparison Test (improper integrals)

Proof with the help of a Large Language Model (AI)

StatementLet $f(x)>0$, $g(x)>0$ for $x\ge a$, integrable on every $[a,R]$, with $\lim_{x\to+\infty}\dfrac{f(x)}{g(x)}=L$ and $0<L<+\infty$. Then $\int_a^{+\infty}f(x)\,dx$ and $\int_a^{+\infty}g(x)\,dx$ converge or diverge together. An analogous test holds for improper integrals of the second kind, with the limit taken at the singular point.
