Properties of the Conjugate

Chapter 0 · Explanation by a Large Language Model and a proof checked by computer (Lean)

StatementProperties of the conjugate of a complex number $z=a+bi$: $\overline{\overline{z}}=z$; $z\in\mathbb{R}\Leftrightarrow\overline{z}=z$ and $z\in i\mathbb{R}$ (purely imaginary) $\Leftrightarrow\overline{z}=-z$; $\operatorname{Re}z=\dfrac{z+\overline{z}}{2}$, $\operatorname{Im}z=\dfrac{z-\overline{z}}{2i}$; $z\overline{z}=|z|^2$; $\overline{z_1+z_2}=\overline{z_1}+\overline{z_2}$, $\overline{z_1 z_2}=\overline{z_1}\,\overline{z_2}$, and $\overline{\left(\dfrac{z_1}{z_2}\right)}=\dfrac{\overline{z_1}}{\overline{z_2}}$ ($z_2\ne 0$).

conj z is the conjugate $\overline{z}$; in the steps Lean writes it as (starRingEnd ℂ) z, which is the same thing. z.re and z.im are the real and imaginary parts, and ‖z‖ is the modulus $|z|$. “Purely imaginary” means $\operatorname{Re}z=0$. For the quotient the hypothesis $z_2\neq 0$ is not needed, since in Lean $\frac{a}{0}=0$.

2. Step by step: $\overline{z_1z_2}$${}=\overline{z_1}\,\overline{z_2}$

In the book: we write $z_1=a+bi$, $z_2=c+di$, carry out the operations and compare real and imaginary parts. In Lean:

The goal

⊢ (starRingEnd ℂ) (z * w) = (starRingEnd ℂ) z * (starRingEnd ℂ) w

import Mathlib

namespace Ch0.Syzygis
open Complex ComplexConjugate

theorem double_conj (z : ℂ) : conj (conj z) = z := conj_conj z

theorem real_iff (z : ℂ) : conj z = z ↔ ∃ r : ℝ, z = r := conj_eq_iff_real

theorem imaginary_iff (z : ℂ) : conj z = -z ↔ z.re = 0 := by
  rw [Complex.ext_iff]
  simp only [conj_re, neg_re, conj_im, neg_im]
  constructor
  · rintro ⟨h, -⟩
    linarith
  · intro h
    exact ⟨by linarith, by simp⟩

theorem re_formula (z : ℂ) : (z.re : ℂ) = (z + conj z) / 2 := re_eq_add_conj z

theorem im_formula (z : ℂ) : (z.im : ℂ) = (z - conj z) / (2 * I) := im_eq_sub_conj z

theorem mul_conj_eq (z : ℂ) : z * conj z = (‖z‖ ^ 2 : ℝ) := by
  rw [mul_conj, normSq_eq_norm_sq]

theorem conj_sum (z w : ℂ) : conj (z + w) = conj z + conj w := by
  apply Complex.ext
  · simp only [conj_re, add_re]
  · simp only [conj_im, add_im]
    ring

theorem conj_product (z w : ℂ) : conj (z * w) = conj z * conj w := by
  apply Complex.ext
  · simp only [conj_re, mul_re, conj_im]
    ring
  · simp only [conj_im, mul_im, conj_re]
    ring

theorem conj_quotient (z w : ℂ) : conj (z / w) = conj z / conj w := map_div₀ conj z w

end Ch0.Syzygis
