1. What is proved: the statement in the book and in Lean

  • Commutativity of addition: $z+w=w+z$
    theorem comm_add (z w : ℂ) : z + w = w + z
  • Commutativity of multiplication: $zw=wz$
    theorem comm_mul (z w : ℂ) : z * w = w * z
  • Associativity of addition
    theorem assoc_add (z w u : ℂ) : z + w + u = z + (w + u)
  • Associativity of multiplication
    theorem assoc_mul (z w u : ℂ) : z * w * u = z * (w * u)
  • Distributivity: $z(w+u)=zw+zu$
    theorem distrib (z w u : ℂ) : z * (w + u) = z * w + z * u
  • Additive identity: $z+0=z$
    theorem zero_neutral (z : ℂ) : z + 0 = z
  • Multiplicative identity: $z\cdot 1=z$
    theorem one_neutral (z : ℂ) : z * 1 = z
  • Additive inverse: $z+(-z)=0$
    theorem opposite (z : ℂ) : z + -z = 0
  • Every $z\neq 0$ has an inverse: $z\,z^{-1}=1$
    theorem inverse (z : ℂ) (hz : z ≠ 0) : z * z⁻¹ = 1
  • The inverse is $z^{-1}=\dfrac{\overline{z}}{|z|^2}$
    theorem inverse_formula (z : ℂ) : z⁻¹ = conj z * ((normSq z)⁻¹ : ℝ)
  • $\mathbb{C}$ is a field
    theorem is_field : IsField ℂ

In Mathlib, $\mathbb{C}$ is defined as pairs of reals $(a,b)$ with the operations of the book, and it is proved to be a field. That is why each property here is a general theorem about fields, applied to $\mathbb{C}$. The expression normSq z is $|z|^2=a^2+b^2$, and conj z is the conjugate $\overline{z}$.

2. Where it is in Mathlib

The field structure of $\mathbb{C}$ in Mathlib:

  • Complex.instField: $\mathbb{C}$ as a field.
  • Complex.inv_def: the formula for the inverse.
  • mul_inv_cancel₀: in every field, $zz^{-1}=1$ for $z\neq 0$.

import Mathlib

namespace Ch0.SomaC
open Complex ComplexConjugate

theorem comm_add (z w : ℂ) : z + w = w + z := add_comm z w

theorem comm_mul (z w : ℂ) : z * w = w * z := mul_comm z w

theorem assoc_add (z w u : ℂ) : z + w + u = z + (w + u) := add_assoc z w u

theorem assoc_mul (z w u : ℂ) : z * w * u = z * (w * u) := mul_assoc z w u

theorem distrib (z w u : ℂ) : z * (w + u) = z * w + z * u := mul_add z w u

theorem zero_neutral (z : ℂ) : z + 0 = z := add_zero z

theorem one_neutral (z : ℂ) : z * 1 = z := mul_one z

theorem opposite (z : ℂ) : z + -z = 0 := add_neg_cancel z

theorem inverse (z : ℂ) (hz : z ≠ 0) : z * z⁻¹ = 1 := mul_inv_cancel₀ hz

theorem inverse_formula (z : ℂ) : z⁻¹ = conj z * ((normSq z)⁻¹ : ℝ) := inv_def z

theorem is_field : IsField ℂ := Field.toIsField ℂ

end Ch0.SomaC
