1. What is proved: the statement in the book and in Lean

  • $i^0=1$
    theorem pow_zero_I : I ^ 0 = 1
  • $i^1=i$
    theorem pow_one_I : I ^ 1 = I
  • $i^2=-1$
    theorem pow_two_I : I ^ 2 = -1
  • $i^3=-i$
    theorem pow_three_I : I ^ 3 = -I
  • $i^4=1$
    theorem pow_four_I : I ^ 4 = 1
  • $i^n=i^{\,n\bmod 4}$
    theorem pow_mod_four (n : ℕ) : I ^ n = I ^ (n % 4)

Here I is the imaginary unit $i$. The expression n % 4 is the remainder of the division of $n$ by $4$ (the $n\bmod 4$ of the book), and n / 4 is the quotient of the integer division.

2. Step by step: $i^n=i^{\,n\bmod 4}$

In the book: we write $n=4q+r$ with $0\le r\lt 4$, so $i^n=(i^4)^q\,i^r=1^q\,i^r=i^r$. In Lean:

The goal
n : ℕ
⊢ I ^ n = I ^ (n % 4)

Above ⊢ is what we know; after ⊢ is what we have to show. After each step we show only what changed.

  1. have h : n = 4 * (n / 4) + n % 4 := (Nat.div_add_mod n 4).symm

    The division identity: $n=4q+r$, with $q=$ n / 4 and $r=$ n % 4 (theorem Nat.div_add_mod).

    What remains to be proved
    h : n = 4 * (n / 4) + n % 4
⊢ I ^ n = I ^ (n % 4)
  2. conv_lhs => rw [h]

    We replace $n$ with $4q+r$ only on the left-hand side. This is done by conv_lhs.

    What remains to be proved
    ⊢ I ^ (4 * (n / 4) + n % 4) = I ^ (n % 4)
  3. rw [pow_add]

    $i^{4q+r}=i^{4q}\cdot i^r$.

    What remains to be proved
    ⊢ I ^ (4 * (n / 4)) * I ^ (n % 4) = I ^ (n % 4)
  4. rw [pow_mul]

    $i^{4q}=(i^4)^q$.

    What remains to be proved
    ⊢ (I ^ 4) ^ (n / 4) * I ^ (n % 4) = I ^ (n % 4)
  5. rw [pow_four_I]

    By the previous theorem, $i^4=1$.

    What remains to be proved
    ⊢ 1 ^ (n / 4) * I ^ (n % 4) = I ^ (n % 4)
  6. rw [one_pow, one_mul]

    $1^q=1$ and $1\cdot i^r=i^r$. The two sides have become identical.

    ✓ No goals: the proof is complete.

import Mathlib

namespace Ch0.DynameisI
open Complex

theorem pow_zero_I : I ^ 0 = 1 := pow_zero I

theorem pow_one_I : I ^ 1 = I := pow_one I

theorem pow_two_I : I ^ 2 = -1 := I_sq

theorem pow_three_I : I ^ 3 = -I := by
  rw [pow_succ, I_sq]
  ring

theorem pow_four_I : I ^ 4 = 1 := by
  rw [pow_succ, pow_three_I]
  rw [neg_mul, ← sq, I_sq, neg_neg]

theorem pow_mod_four (n : ℕ) : I ^ n = I ^ (n % 4) := by
  have h : n = 4 * (n / 4) + n % 4 := (Nat.div_add_mod n 4).symm
  conv_lhs => rw [h]
  rw [pow_add]
  rw [pow_mul]
  rw [pow_four_I]
  rw [one_pow, one_mul]

end Ch0.DynameisI
