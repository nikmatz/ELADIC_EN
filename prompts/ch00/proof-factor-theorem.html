StatementFactor Theorem: for a polynomial $P$ of degree $n\ge 1$, $r$ is a root ($P(r)=0$) $\Leftrightarrow$ $(x-r)$ divides $P(x)$, that is, $P(x)=(x-r)Q(x)$ with $Q$ of degree $n-1$.
