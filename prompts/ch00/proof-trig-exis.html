General Solution of the Basic Trigonometric Equations

Proof with the help of a Large Language Model (AI)

StatementGeneral solutions of the basic trigonometric equations (with $\kappa\in\mathbb{Z}$): $\sin x=k$ (for $|k|\le 1$) $\Rightarrow x=\arcsin k+2\kappa\pi$ or $x=\pi-\arcsin k+2\kappa\pi$; $\cos x=k$ (for $|k|\le 1$) $\Rightarrow x=\pm\arccos k+2\kappa\pi$; $\tan x=k\Rightarrow x=\arctan k+\kappa\pi$. If $|k|\gt 1$, the equations $\sin x=k$ and $\cos x=k$ have no solution.
