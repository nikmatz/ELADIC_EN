Properties of Inequalities

StatementProperties of inequalities in the reals: if $a\lt b$ then $a+c\lt b+c$; if $a\lt b$ and $c\gt 0$ then $ac\lt bc$, whereas if $c\lt 0$ then $ac\gt bc$; transitivity: if $a\lt b$ and $b\lt c$ then $a\lt c$; and reciprocals: if $0\lt a\lt b$ then $\dfrac{1}{a}\gt\dfrac{1}{b}$.
