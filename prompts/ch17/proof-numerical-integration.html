Numerical Integration (Trapezoidal & Simpson)

Proof with the help of a Large Language Model (AI)

StatementFor $\int_a^b f\,dx$ with $n$ subintervals and $h=\frac{b-a}{n}$: Trapezoidal rule $T_n=\dfrac{h}{2}[f(x_0)+2f(x_1)+\dots+2f(x_{n-1})+f(x_n)]$, with error $\le\dfrac{(b-a)h^2 M_2}{12}$. Simpson's rule ($n$ even) $S_n=\dfrac{h}{3}[f(x_0)+4f(x_1)+2f(x_2)+\dots+f(x_n)]$, with error $\le\dfrac{(b-a)h^4 M_4}{180}$, where $M_2=\max_{[a,b]}|f''(x)|$ and $M_4=\max_{[a,b]}|f^{(4)}(x)|$ ($f''$ must be continuous for the trapezoidal rule and $f^{(4)}$ continuous for Simpson's).
