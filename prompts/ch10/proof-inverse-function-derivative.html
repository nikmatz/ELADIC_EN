Derivative of the Inverse Function

Chapter 10 · Explanation by a Large Language Model and a proof checked by computer (Lean)

StatementIf $f$ is differentiable on a neighborhood of $x_0$, invertible, and $f'(x_0)\ne 0$, then $(f^{-1})'(y_0)=\dfrac{1}{f'(x_0)}$ where $y_0=f(x_0)$. For example, $(\arcsin x)'=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}$ for $|x|<1$, and $(\arctan x)'=\dfrac{1}{1+x^2}$.

1. What is proved: the statement in the book and in Lean

  • If $g$ is the inverse of $f$ near $y_0$ and continuous at $y_0$, and $f$ has derivative $f'\neq 0$ at $x_0=g(y_0)$, then $g'(y_0)=\dfrac{1}{f'}$
    theorem inverse_deriv (f g : ℝ → ℝ) (y₀ f' : ℝ) (hg : ContinuousAt g y₀)
    (hf : HasDerivAt f f' (g y₀)) (hf0 : f' ≠ 0) (hfg : ∀ᶠ y in 𝓝 y₀, f (g y) = y) :
    HasDerivAt g (1 / f') y₀
  • $(\arcsin x)'$${}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}$ for $|x|\lt 1$
    theorem arcsin_deriv (x : ℝ) (h : |x| < 1) :
    HasDerivAt Real.arcsin (1 / √(1 - x ^ 2)) x
  • $(\arctan x)'=\dfrac{1}{1+x^2}$
    theorem arctan_deriv (x : ℝ) : HasDerivAt Real.arctan (1 / (1 + x ^ 2)) x

The inverse $f^{-1}$ is written $g$: the hypothesis hfg says that $f(g(y))=y$ for every $y$ near $y_0$. The point $x_0$ of the book is $x_0=g(y_0)$, so $y_0=f(x_0)$. We also need the continuity of $g$ at $y_0$ (hypothesis hg); in the setting of the book it always holds, because the inverse of a continuous invertible function on an interval is continuous. Real.arcsin and Real.arctan are the inverse trigonometric functions of Mathlib.

2. Where it is in Mathlib

We used the following theorems:

  • HasDerivAt.of_local_left_inverse: the derivative of the inverse function.
  • Real.hasDerivAt_arcsin: the derivative of $\arcsin x$, for $x\neq\pm 1$.
  • Real.hasDerivAt_arctan: the derivative of $\arctan x$.
  • abs_lt: $|x|\lt 1$ exactly when $-1\lt x\lt 1$.

import Mathlib

namespace Ch10.AntistrofiParagogos
open Filter Topology

theorem inverse_deriv (f g : ℝ → ℝ) (y₀ f' : ℝ) (hg : ContinuousAt g y₀)
    (hf : HasDerivAt f f' (g y₀)) (hf0 : f' ≠ 0) (hfg : ∀ᶠ y in 𝓝 y₀, f (g y) = y) :
    HasDerivAt g (1 / f') y₀ := by
  rw [one_div]
  exact hf.of_local_left_inverse hg hf0 hfg

theorem arcsin_deriv (x : ℝ) (h : |x| < 1) :
    HasDerivAt Real.arcsin (1 / √(1 - x ^ 2)) x := by
  rw [abs_lt] at h
  exact Real.hasDerivAt_arcsin h.1.ne' h.2.ne

theorem arctan_deriv (x : ℝ) : HasDerivAt Real.arctan (1 / (1 + x ^ 2)) x :=
  Real.hasDerivAt_arctan x

end Ch10.AntistrofiParagogos
