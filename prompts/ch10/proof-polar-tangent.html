Slope of the Tangent Line to a Polar Curve

Proof with the help of a Large Language Model (AI)

StatementFor a polar curve $r=f(\theta)$ (with $r'=dr/d\theta$): $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac{r'\sin\theta+r\cos\theta}{r'\cos\theta-r\sin\theta}$. The tangent line is horizontal if $r'\sin\theta+r\cos\theta=0$ and $r'\cos\theta-r\sin\theta\ne 0$, and vertical if $r'\cos\theta-r\sin\theta=0$ and $r'\sin\theta+r\cos\theta\ne 0$. When both vanish (the form $0/0$, for instance at the cusp of the cardioid) the test is inconclusive and a limit or a higher-order expansion is needed.
