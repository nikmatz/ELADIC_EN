Question 1 / 10

Integration by parts: $\int u\,dv=$

✓ Answer: b) $uv-\int v\,du$
$\int u\,dv=uv-\int v\,du$ (the integration by parts formula).
Question 2 / 10

$\int x e^x\,dx=$

✓ Answer: c) $xe^x-e^x+C$
$u=x$, $dv=e^xdx$: $\int xe^xdx=xe^x-\int e^xdx=xe^x-e^x+C=(x-1)e^x+C$.
Question 3 / 10

For $\int\dfrac{dx}{x^2-1}$ we use partial fractions.

✓ Answer: Correct
$\frac{1}{x^2-1}=\frac{1}{(x-1)(x+1)}=\frac{A}{x-1}+\frac{B}{x+1}$: partial fractions.
Question 4 / 10

$\int\dfrac{1}{x^2+1}dx=$

✓ Answer: b) $\arctan x+C$
A basic integral: $\int\frac{dx}{x^2+1}=\arctan x+C$.
Question 5 / 10

For $\int\sqrt{1-x^2}\,dx$ we use:

✓ Answer: b) $x=\sin\theta$
$x=\sin\theta$: $1-x^2=1-\sin^2\theta=\cos^2\theta$, which simplifies the square root.
Question 6 / 10

$\int\ln x\,dx=$

✓ Answer: c) $x\ln x-x+C$
By parts with $u=\ln x$, $dv=dx$: $\int\ln x\,dx=x\ln x-\int x\cdot\frac{1}{x}dx=x\ln x-x+C$.
Question 7 / 10

$\int\dfrac{P(x)}{Q(x)}dx$ with $\deg P\geq\deg Q$: we first perform polynomial division.

✓ Answer: Correct
If the degree of the denominator is ≤ that of the numerator, divide first and then use partial fractions.
Question 8 / 10

$\int\dfrac{2x}{(x^2+1)^2}dx=$

✓ Answer: b) $\dfrac{-1}{x^2+1}+C$
$u=x^2+1$, $du=2x\,dx$: $\int\frac{2x}{(x^2+1)^2}dx=\int u^{-2}du=-u^{-1}+C=\frac{-1}{x^2+1}+C$.
Question 9 / 10

$\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin^2x\,dx=$

✓ Answer: b) $\pi/4$
$\sin^2x=\frac{1-\cos2x}{2}$: $\int_0^{\pi/2}\frac{1-\cos2x}{2}dx=\left[\frac{x}{2}-\frac{\sin2x}{4}\right]_0^{\pi/2}=\frac{\pi}{4}$.
Question 10 / 10

The method of partial fractions applies only to rational functions.

✓ Answer: Correct
Partial fractions: the decomposition of a rational function $P(x)/Q(x)$ into simpler fractions.
