Limits and Continuity

Chapter 9 · Limits — Continuity

Self-Assessment

10 questions on computing limits, indeterminate forms, continuity and the related theorems.

Question 1 / 10

$\lim_{x\to2}(3x^2-1)=$

✓ Answer: a) 11
Direct substitution: $3(2)^2-1=12-1=11$.
Question 2 / 10

$\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\sin x}{x}=$

✓ Answer: c) 1
The well-known fundamental limit: $\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\sin x}{x}=1$.
Question 3 / 10

If $\lim_{x\to a}f(x)=L$ and $\lim_{x\to a}g(x)=0$, then $\lim_{x\to a}\dfrac{f(x)}{g(x)}=\infty$.

✓ Answer: Incorrect
If $L\neq0$ and $g\to0^+$, then $f/g\to\pm\infty$. But if $L=0$ as well, we get $0/0$, an indeterminate form.
Question 4 / 10

$\lim_{x\to\infty}\dfrac{3x^2+5}{x^2-1}=$

✓ Answer: c) 3
Dividing by $x^2$: $\dfrac{3+5/x^2}{1-1/x^2}\to\dfrac{3}{1}=3$.
Question 5 / 10

Which condition describes the continuity of $f$ at $a$?

✓ Answer: c) $\lim_{x\to a}f(x)=f(a)$
$f$ is continuous at $a$ $\Leftrightarrow$: (1) $f(a)$ is defined, (2) $\lim_{x\to a}f(x)$ exists, (3) $\lim_{x\to a}f(x)=f(a)$.
Question 6 / 10

The function $f(x)=|x|$ is continuous for every $x\in\mathbb{R}$.

✓ Answer: Correct
$|x|$ is continuous everywhere: its graph is a V with no breaks.
Question 7 / 10

Intermediate Value Theorem: if $f$ is continuous on $[a,b]$ and $f(a)<0<f(b)$, then:

✓ Answer: b) there exists $c\in(a,b)$ with $f(c)=0$
The IVT guarantees the existence of some $c\in(a,b)$ with $f(c)=0$. It says nothing about how many such points there are or where they lie.
Question 8 / 10

$\lim_{x\to0^+}\ln x=$

✓ Answer: c) $-\infty$
As $x\to0^+$, $\ln x\to-\infty$.
Question 9 / 10

Every function that is differentiable at $a$ is continuous at $a$.

✓ Answer: Correct
Differentiability $\Rightarrow$ continuity (the converse is false, e.g. $f(x)=|x|$ at 0).
Question 10 / 10

$\lim_{x\to1}\dfrac{x^2-1}{x-1}=$

✓ Answer: c) 2
$\dfrac{x^2-1}{x-1}=\dfrac{(x-1)(x+1)}{x-1}=x+1\to2$ as $x\to1$.
