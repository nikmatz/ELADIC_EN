Monotonicity, Convexity and Extrema

Chapter 11 · Analysis of Functions

Self-Assessment

10 questions on monotonicity, extrema, convexity, the Weierstrass and Mean Value theorems, and the Taylor polynomial.

Question 1 / 10

If $f'(x)>0$ on $(a,b)$, then $f$:

✓ Answer: b) is increasing
$f'>0$ $\Rightarrow$ $f$ is strictly increasing on $(a,b)$.
Question 2 / 10

If $f'(c)=0$, then $c$ is necessarily a local extremum.

✓ Answer: Incorrect
$f'(c)=0$ is a necessary but not a sufficient condition. For example, $f(x)=x^3$ at $x=0$ has $f'(0)=0$, yet this is an inflection point.
Question 3 / 10

Second derivative test: if $f'(c)=0$ and $f''(c)>0$, then $c$ is:

✓ Answer: b) a local minimum
$f'(c)=0$ together with $f''(c)>0$ $\Rightarrow$ a local minimum (the curve is concave up).
Question 4 / 10

If $f''(x)>0$ on $(a,b)$, then $f$:

✓ Answer: b) is convex
$f''>0$ $\Rightarrow$ $f'$ is increasing $\Rightarrow$ the graph is convex (concave up).
Question 5 / 10

At an inflection point, $f''$ changes sign.

✓ Answer: Correct
Inflection point: a change of convexity/concavity $\Leftrightarrow$ $f''$ changes sign.
Question 6 / 10

The Taylor polynomial $P_n(x)$ of $f$ about $x=a$ satisfies:

✓ Answer: b) $P_n^{(k)}(a)=f^{(k)}(a)$ for $k=0,\ldots,n$
The Taylor polynomial has the same derivatives as $f$ up to order $n$ at $a$.
Question 7 / 10

The function $f(x)=x^4-4x^2$ has local extrema at:

✓ Answer: c) $x=0$ and $x=\pm\sqrt{2}$
$f'=4x^3-8x=4x(x^2-2)=0$: $x=0,\pm\sqrt{2}$. Second derivative test: $f''(0)=-8<0$ (maximum), $f''(\pm\sqrt{2})=16>0$ (minima).
Question 8 / 10

If $f$ is continuous on $[a,b]$, it attains an absolute maximum and an absolute minimum on $[a,b]$.

✓ Answer: Correct
Weierstrass' theorem: continuous on a closed bounded interval $\Rightarrow$ the absolute max/min are attained.
Question 9 / 10

Mean Value Theorem: if $f$ is continuous on $[a,b]$ and differentiable on $(a,b)$, then there exists $c$ with:

✓ Answer: b) $f'(c)=\dfrac{f(b)-f(a)}{b-a}$
MVT (Lagrange): $\exists c\in(a,b)$ with $f'(c)=\dfrac{f(b)-f(a)}{b-a}$ (the tangent line is parallel to the chord).
Question 10 / 10

If $f'(x)=0$ for every $x$ in $(a,b)$, then $f$:

✓ Answer: c) is constant
$f'=0$ everywhere $\Rightarrow$ $f$ is constant (by the MVT).
