10 questions on the domain, composition, the inverse function, symmetries and transformations of graphs, as well as the basic families of functions.

Question 1 / 10

The domain of $f(x)=\sqrt{x-2}$ is:

✓ Answer: b) $x\geq2$
$\sqrt{x-2}$ is defined when $x-2\geq0$, i.e. $x\geq2$. Domain: $[2,+\infty)$.
Question 2 / 10

Every function that is one-to-one and onto has an inverse.

✓ Answer: Correct
An inverse function exists if and only if $f$ is one-to-one and onto (bijective).
Question 3 / 10

If $f(x)=2x+1$ and $g(x)=x^2$, then $(f\circ g)(x)=$

✓ Answer: a) $2x^2+1$
$(f\circ g)(x)=f(g(x))=f(x^2)=2x^2+1$.
Question 4 / 10

Which of the following functions is odd?

✓ Answer: b) $f(x)=x^3$
$f(-x)=-x^3=-f(x)$: odd. $x^2$: even. $e^x$ and $x^2+1$: neither even nor odd.
Question 5 / 10

The function $f(x)=\ln x$ is defined at $x=0$.

✓ Answer: Incorrect
$\ln x$ is defined only for $x>0$. At $x=0$ it is undefined ($\lim_{x\to0^+}\ln x=-\infty$).
Question 6 / 10

The inverse of $f(x)=e^x$ is:

✓ Answer: a) $f^{-1}(x)=\ln x$
$y=e^x \Rightarrow x=\ln y$, so $f^{-1}(x)=\ln x$ (for $x>0$).
Question 7 / 10

The graph of $f(-x)$ is:

✓ Answer: b) The reflection about the $Oy$ axis
The substitution $x\to -x$ reflects the graph about the $Oy$ axis (the vertical axis).
Question 8 / 10

Every strictly increasing function is one-to-one.

✓ Answer: Correct
If $x_1<x_2 \Rightarrow f(x_1)<f(x_2)$, then $f(x_1)\neq f(x_2)$ whenever $x_1\neq x_2$: one-to-one.
Question 9 / 10

The domain of $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{x^2-4}$ is:

✓ Answer: c) $\mathbb{R}\setminus\{-2,2\}$
The denominator vanishes at $x=\pm2$. Hence $D_f=\mathbb{R}\setminus\{-2,2\}$.
Question 10 / 10

If $f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ with $f(x)=x^3$, then:

✓ Answer: c) $f$ is bijective (one-to-one and onto)
$x^3$ is strictly increasing (one-to-one) and its range is ℝ (onto). Hence it is bijective, with inverse $f^{-1}(x)=x^{1/3}$.
