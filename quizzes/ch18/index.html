10 questions on the convergence of improper integrals and convergence tests.

Question 1 / 10

$\int_1^{+\infty}\dfrac{1}{x^2}dx=$

✓ Answer: b) $1$
$\int_1^\infty x^{-2}dx=\left[-x^{-1}\right]_1^\infty=0-(-1)=1$.
Question 2 / 10

$\int_1^{+\infty}\dfrac{1}{x}dx$ converges.

✓ Answer: Incorrect
$\int_1^R\frac{dx}{x}=\ln R\to+\infty$: it diverges.
Question 3 / 10

$\int_1^{+\infty}\dfrac{1}{x^p}dx$ converges if and only if:

✓ Answer: b) $p>1$
$\int_1^\infty x^{-p}dx$ converges $\Leftrightarrow$ $p>1$.
Question 4 / 10

$\int_0^{+\infty}e^{-x}dx=$

✓ Answer: c) 1
$\int_0^\infty e^{-x}dx=[-e^{-x}]_0^\infty=0-(-1)=1$.
Question 5 / 10

Comparison test: if $0\leq f\leq g$ and $\int_a^\infty g$ converges, then $\int_a^\infty f$ converges.

✓ Answer: Correct
Comparison test (upper bound): smaller than a convergent one $\Rightarrow$ it converges.
Question 6 / 10

$\int_0^1\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x}}dx=$

✓ Answer: c) $2$
$\int_0^1 x^{-1/2}dx=[2\sqrt{x}]_0^1=2-0=2$. (Improper: $f\to\infty$ at 0, but it converges.)
Question 7 / 10

$\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}e^{-x^2}dx=$

✓ Answer: c) $\sqrt{\pi}$
Gaussian integral: $\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}e^{-x^2}dx=\sqrt{\pi}$ (a classical result).
Question 8 / 10

$\int_{-1}^{1}\dfrac{1}{x}dx$ diverges.

✓ Answer: Correct
$1/x$ has a vertical asymptote at $x=0\in(-1,1)$: $\int_{-1}^0+\int_0^1$ both diverge.
Question 9 / 10

Limit comparison test: if $\lim_{x\to\infty}\dfrac{f(x)}{g(x)}=L\in(0,\infty)$, then:

✓ Answer: b) $\int f$ and $\int g$ converge or diverge together
Limit comparison test: $0<L<\infty$ $\Rightarrow$ $\int_a^\infty f$ and $\int_a^\infty g$ behave the same way.
Question 10 / 10

$\int_0^{+\infty}xe^{-x}dx=$

✓ Answer: c) 1
$\int_0^\infty xe^{-x}dx=\Gamma(2)=1!=1$ (by parts: $[-xe^{-x}]_0^\infty+\int_0^\infty e^{-x}dx=0+1=1$).
