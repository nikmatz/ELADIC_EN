10 questions on basic integrals, their properties and the method of substitution.

Question 1 / 10

$\int x^3\,dx=$

✓ Answer: c) $\dfrac{x^4}{4}+C$
$\int x^n dx=\dfrac{x^{n+1}}{n+1}+C$ (for $n\neq-1$). Hence $\int x^3 dx=\dfrac{x^4}{4}+C$.
Question 2 / 10

$\int\dfrac{1}{x}\,dx = \ln|x|+C$.

✓ Answer: Correct
A basic integral: $\int\frac{1}{x}dx=\ln|x|+C$ (for $x\neq0$).
Question 3 / 10

$\int e^{2x}\,dx=$

✓ Answer: c) $\dfrac{e^{2x}}{2}+C$
$\int e^{ax}dx=\dfrac{e^{ax}}{a}+C$. For $a=2$: $\dfrac{e^{2x}}{2}+C$.
Question 4 / 10

If $F'(x)=f(x)$, then $F$ is called:

✓ Answer: b) an antiderivative (primitive)
An antiderivative satisfies $F'(x)=f(x)$. The indefinite integral is $\int f = F+C$.
Question 5 / 10

Two antiderivatives of the same $f$ differ by a constant.

✓ Answer: Correct
If $F'=G'=f$, then $(F-G)'=0$, so $F-G=C$ (a constant).
Question 6 / 10

$\int\cos(3x)\,dx=$

✓ Answer: c) $\dfrac{\sin(3x)}{3}+C$
$\int\cos(ax)dx=\dfrac{\sin(ax)}{a}+C$. For $a=3$: $\dfrac{\sin(3x)}{3}+C$.
Question 7 / 10

Method of substitution: with $u=g(x)$, the integral $\int f(g(x))g'(x)dx$ becomes:

✓ Answer: a) $\int f(u)du$
$u=g(x)$, $du=g'(x)dx$: $\int f(g(x))g'(x)dx=\int f(u)du$.
Question 8 / 10

$\int(5x^2-3x+2)\,dx=$

✓ Answer: b) $\dfrac{5x^3}{3}-\dfrac{3x^2}{2}+2x+C$
By linearity: $\int(5x^2-3x+2)dx=\frac{5x^3}{3}-\frac{3x^2}{2}+2x+C$.
Question 9 / 10

$\int\sin x\,dx = -\cos x+C$.

✓ Answer: Correct
$(\cos x)'=-\sin x$, so $(-\cos x)'=\sin x$: $\int\sin x\,dx=-\cos x+C$.
Question 10 / 10

$\int\dfrac{2x}{x^2+1}\,dx=$

✓ Answer: b) $\ln(x^2+1)+C$
$u=x^2+1$, $du=2x\,dx$: $\int\frac{2x}{x^2+1}dx=\int\frac{du}{u}=\ln|u|+C=\ln(x^2+1)+C$.
