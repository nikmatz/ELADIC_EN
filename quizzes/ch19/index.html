10 questions on the convergence of sequences, series and convergence tests.

Question 1 / 10

$\lim_{n\to\infty}\dfrac{1}{n}=$

✓ Answer: d) 0
$1/n\to0$ as $n\to\infty$.
Question 2 / 10

Every decreasing sequence that is bounded below converges.

✓ Answer: Correct
Monotone and bounded $\Rightarrow$ it converges (the monotone convergence theorem).
Question 3 / 10

The geometric series $\sum_{n=0}^\infty ar^n$ converges if:

✓ Answer: b) $|r|<1$
Geometric series: it converges $\Leftrightarrow$ $|r|<1$, and then $\sum=\dfrac{a}{1-r}$.
Question 4 / 10

D'Alembert's test (ratio test): if $\lim_{n\to\infty}\left|\dfrac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}\right|=L<1$, then $\sum a_n$:

✓ Answer: b) converges absolutely
D'Alembert: $L<1\Rightarrow$ absolute convergence. $L>1\Rightarrow$ divergence. $L=1\Rightarrow$ inconclusive.
Question 5 / 10

If $\sum a_n$ converges, then $a_n\to0$.

✓ Answer: Correct
Necessary condition for convergence: $\lim_{n\to\infty}a_n=0$. If $a_n\not\to0$, the series diverges.
Question 6 / 10

$\sum_{n=1}^\infty\dfrac{1}{n^2}$ is:

✓ Answer: b) A $p$-series with $p=2>1$ — converges
$p$-series $\sum\frac{1}{n^p}$: it converges if $p>1$. For $p=2$: $\sum\frac{1}{n^2}=\frac{\pi^2}{6}$.
Question 7 / 10

The harmonic series $\sum_{n=1}^\infty\dfrac{1}{n}$:

✓ Answer: c) Diverges
The harmonic series $\sum\frac{1}{n}$ diverges (historical note: Oresme, 14th century).
Question 8 / 10

Integral test: $\sum_{n=1}^\infty f(n)$ and $\int_1^\infty f(x)dx$ behave the same way (convergence/divergence).

✓ Answer: Correct
Integral test (Cauchy-Maclaurin): it holds if $f$ is positive, decreasing and continuous.
Question 9 / 10

The alternating series $\sum_{n=1}^\infty\dfrac{(-1)^{n+1}}{n}$ converges to:

✓ Answer: a) $\ln 2$
Leibniz test + Taylor: $\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac{(-1)^{n+1}}{n}=\ln 2$.
Question 10 / 10

$\sum_{n=0}^\infty\dfrac{1}{2^n}=$

✓ Answer: b) 2
Geometric: $a=1$, $r=1/2$, $|r|<1$: $\sum=\frac{1}{1-1/2}=2$.
