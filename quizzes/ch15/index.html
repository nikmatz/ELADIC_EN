Question 1 / 10

FTC (part 1): if $F(x)=\int_a^x f(t)dt$, then $F'(x)=$

✓ Answer: b) $f(x)$
Part 1 of the FTC: $\dfrac{d}{dx}\int_a^xf(t)dt=f(x)$ (if $f$ is continuous).
Question 2 / 10

FTC (part 2): $\int_a^b f(x)dx=$

✓ Answer: b) $F(b)-F(a)$
Part 2 of the FTC: $\int_a^bf(x)dx=F(b)-F(a)$, where $F'=f$.
Question 3 / 10

The FTC connects differentiation with integration.

✓ Answer: Correct
The fundamental connection: integration and differentiation are inverse operations.
Question 4 / 10

$\dfrac{d}{dx}\int_0^{x^2}\cos t\,dt=$

✓ Answer: b) $2x\cos(x^2)$
Chain rule + FTC: $\dfrac{d}{dx}\int_0^{g(x)}f(t)dt=f(g(x))\cdot g'(x)=\cos(x^2)\cdot2x$.
Question 5 / 10

$\int_1^3(2x+1)dx=$

✓ Answer: b) 10
$[x^2+x]_1^3=(9+3)-(1+1)=12-2=10$. Hence 10.
Question 6 / 10

The function $F(x)=\int_a^xf(t)dt$ is an antiderivative of $f$.

✓ Answer: Correct
By part 1 of the FTC: $F'(x)=f(x)$, so $F$ is an antiderivative.
Question 7 / 10

$\int_0^1 e^x\,dx=$

✓ Answer: a) $e-1$
$[e^x]_0^1=e^1-e^0=e-1$.
Question 8 / 10

If $f$ is continuous and $\int_0^3f(x)dx=7$, $\int_0^2f(x)dx=3$, then $\int_2^3f(x)dx=$

✓ Answer: a) 4
By additivity: $\int_0^3=\int_0^2+\int_2^3 \Rightarrow \int_2^3=7-3=4$.
Question 9 / 10

$\int_a^b f(x)dx = \int_a^b f(t)dt$ (the variable of integration is a dummy variable).

✓ Answer: Correct
The name of the variable of integration (a dummy variable) does not affect the result.
Question 10 / 10

$\dfrac{d}{dx}\int_x^5\sin(t^2)dt=$

✓ Answer: b) $-\sin(x^2)$
$\int_x^5=-\int_5^x$, so $\dfrac{d}{dx}(-\int_5^x\sin t^2 dt)=-\sin(x^2)$.
