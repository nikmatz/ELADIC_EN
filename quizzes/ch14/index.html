10 questions on Riemann sums, the properties of the definite integral and its geometric interpretation.

Question 1 / 10

$\int_0^2 3x^2\,dx=$

✓ Answer: c) 8
$\int_0^2 3x^2dx=[x^3]_0^2=8-0=8$.
Question 2 / 10

$\int_a^b f(x)\,dx = -\int_b^a f(x)\,dx$.

✓ Answer: Correct
A property of the integral: interchanging the limits changes the sign.
Question 3 / 10

If $f(x)\geq0$ on $[a,b]$, then $\int_a^b f(x)\,dx$:

✓ Answer: b) is the area between $f$ and the $x$-axis
For $f\geq0$ the definite integral equals the area under the graph of $f$.
Question 4 / 10

$\int_a^a f(x)\,dx=$

✓ Answer: c) 0
When the two limits of integration coincide, the integral is 0.
Question 5 / 10

$\int_{-1}^{1} x^3\,dx = 0$, because $x^3$ is an odd function.

✓ Answer: Correct
For an odd $f$: $\int_{-a}^{a}f(x)dx=0$.
Question 6 / 10

Which of the following properties of the definite integral hold?

✓ Answer: d) All of the above
All of them hold: linearity, additivity with respect to the limits, and homogeneity.
Question 7 / 10

The Riemann sum $\sum_{i=1}^n f(x_i^*)\Delta x_i$ represents:

✓ Answer: b) an approximation of the area by rectangles
A Riemann sum approximates the area by $n$ rectangles. In the limit ($n\to\infty$) it gives the exact integral.
Question 8 / 10

$\int_0^\pi \sin x\,dx=$

✓ Answer: c) 2
$[-\cos x]_0^\pi=-\cos\pi+\cos0=1+1=2$.
Question 9 / 10

If $f\leq g$ on $[a,b]$, then $\int_a^bf\leq\int_a^bg$.

✓ Answer: Correct
Monotonicity of the integral: $f\leq g$ $\Rightarrow$ $\int_a^bf(x)dx\leq\int_a^bg(x)dx$.
Question 10 / 10

The average value of $f$ on $[a,b]$ is:

✓ Answer: c) $\dfrac{1}{b-a}\int_a^bf(x)dx$
Average value: $\bar f=\dfrac{1}{b-a}\int_a^bf(x)dx$.
