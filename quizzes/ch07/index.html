10 questions on linear dynamical systems, stability, Markov chains and phase portraits.

Question 1 / 10

In a discrete dynamical system $x_{k+1}=Ax_k$, the general solution is:

✓ Answer: a) $x_k=A^kx_0$
$x_1=Ax_0$, $x_2=Ax_1=A^2x_0$, and so on. Hence $x_k=A^kx_0$.
Question 2 / 10

If all the eigenvalues of $A$ satisfy $|\lambda_i|<1$, the system $x_{k+1}=Ax_k$ is asymptotically stable.

✓ Answer: Correct
$|\lambda_i|<1$ $\Rightarrow$ $A^k\to 0$ as $k\to\infty$, so $x_k\to 0$.
Question 3 / 10

In a Markov chain, each column of the transition matrix $P$ sums to:

✓ Answer: c) 1
Each column represents transition probabilities, so its entries sum to 1 ($P$ is stochastic).
Question 4 / 10

The identity matrix $I$ is a valid transition matrix of a Markov chain — it never changes the state.

✓ Answer: Correct
$I$ is stochastic (each column has a single 1 and the rest 0). Every state stays where it is.
Question 5 / 10

The stationary distribution $\pi$ of a Markov chain satisfies:

✓ Answer: b) $P\pi=\pi$
Stationary means $P\pi=\pi$, i.e. $\pi$ is an eigenvector for the eigenvalue $\lambda=1$.
Question 6 / 10

If the eigenvalues in $x_{k+1}=Ax_k$ are $\lambda_1=1$ and $\lambda_2=0.5$, then in the long run the system:

✓ Answer: b) tends to the direction of the eigenvector for $\lambda_1=1$
The term $\lambda_2^k=0.5^k\to0$, so the term $\lambda_1^k=1^k=1$ dominates: convergence along the direction of $\lambda_1$.
Question 7 / 10

In the phase portrait of a fixed point, if the eigenvalues satisfy $\lambda<0<\mu$, we have a saddle.

✓ Answer: Correct
Eigenvalues of opposite signs give an unstable saddle. One eigenspace is attracting, the other repelling.
Question 8 / 10

If $|\lambda|>1$ for some eigenvalue, the discrete system is:

✓ Answer: b) unstable
$|\lambda|>1$ $\Rightarrow$ $|\lambda|^k\to\infty$, so the system diverges (it is unstable).
Question 9 / 10

In a Markov chain, what does $(P^k)_{ij}$ represent?

✓ Answer: a) The probability of going from $j$ to $i$ in $k$ steps
$(P^k)_{ij}$ = the probability of being in state $i$ after $k$ steps, starting from $j$.
Question 10 / 10

Every regular Markov chain has a unique stationary distribution.

✓ Answer: Correct
Theorem: a regular Markov chain (some power has all entries >0) $\Rightarrow$ a unique stationary distribution.
