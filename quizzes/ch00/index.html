12 questions on number sets, inequalities, polynomials, analytic geometry, trigonometry, complex numbers and linear systems.

Question 1 / 12

Every rational number is a real number.

✓ Answer: Correct
The sets satisfy ℕ⊂ℤ⊂ℚ⊂ℝ. Every rational q = m/n belongs to ℝ.
Question 2 / 12

Which of the following does NOT belong to ℚ?

✓ Answer: d) $\sqrt{2}$
$\sqrt{2}$ is irrational (proof by contradiction: if $\sqrt{2}=p/q$, then 2q²=p², a contradiction). The rest belong to ℚ.
Question 3 / 12

The solution of the inequality $|x-3|<2$ is:

✓ Answer: b) $1<x<5$
$|x-3|<2 \Leftrightarrow -2<x-3<2 \Leftrightarrow 1<x<5$.
Question 4 / 12

The equation of the line with slope $m=2$ passing through $(1,3)$ is:

✓ Answer: a) $y=2x+1$
$y-3=2(x-1) \Rightarrow y=2x+1$.
Question 5 / 12

The inner product $\mathbf{u}\cdot\mathbf{v}=|\mathbf{u}||\mathbf{v}|\cos\theta$ equals zero when the vectors are parallel.

✓ Answer: Incorrect
It vanishes when the vectors are perpendicular (θ=90°). For parallel vectors cosθ=±1, so the product is NOT zero.
Question 6 / 12

The product $(x+3)(x-3)$ equals:

✓ Answer: c) $x^2-9$
$(a+b)(a-b)=a^2-b^2$, so $(x+3)(x-3)=x^2-9$.
Question 7 / 12

How many roots, counted with multiplicity, does the polynomial $p(x)=x^3-3x+2$ have in ℝ?

✓ Answer: d) 3
$p(x)=(x-1)^2(x+2)$, so the roots are $x=1$ (double) and $x=-2$. A total of 3 roots (with multiplicity).
Question 8 / 12

$\sin(\pi/6) = 1/2$.

✓ Answer: Correct
From the unit circle: $\sin 30° = \sin(\pi/6) = 1/2$.
Question 9 / 12

The modulus of the complex number $z = 3-4i$ is:

✓ Answer: b) 5
$|z|=\sqrt{3^2+(-4)^2}=\sqrt{9+16}=\sqrt{25}=5$.
Question 10 / 12

If $z=1+i$, what is $z^2$?

✓ Answer: b) $2i$
$z^2=(1+i)^2=1+2i+i^2=1+2i-1=2i$.
Question 11 / 12

The solution of a $2\times2$ linear system whose coefficient matrix has determinant ≠ 0 is unique.

✓ Answer: Correct
If $\det A\neq 0$, the matrix is invertible and the system $Ax=b$ has the unique solution $x=A^{-1}b$.
Question 12 / 12

What is the value of $\cos(2\pi/3)$?

✓ Answer: b) $-\dfrac{1}{2}$
$\cos(120°)=\cos(180°-60°)=-\cos(60°)=-1/2$.
