Volume of Revolution (Disks)

Chapter 17 · Applications of the Integral · The disk method

The disk method: the region under $y=f(x)$ on $[a,b]$ is revolved about the $x$-axis. Each thin strip becomes a disk of radius $f(x)$.

Volume of a Solid of Revolution

With the disk method the volume is approximated by a Riemann sum of disk volumes $\pi f(x_i)^2\,\Delta x$, and in the limit:

$V=\pi\displaystyle\int_a^b \big(f(x)\big)^2\,dx$

Each disk has area $\pi r^2=\pi f(x_i)^2$ and thickness $\Delta x$. As $n\to\infty$ the sum converges to the integral.
