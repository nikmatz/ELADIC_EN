Newton–Leibniz: the integral as a difference of antiderivative values

∫ab f(x) dx = F(b) − F(a)

The idea: The area under f on [a,b] (blue region) does not require infinitely many rectangles — it is simply the difference of two values of the antiderivative F. The two orange dots on the right-hand diagram are F(a) and F(b); the height between them is the integral.
