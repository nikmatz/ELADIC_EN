Riemann Sums & the Definite Integral

The definite integral ∫ₐᵇf(x)dx is defined as a limit of Riemann sums.

Sₙ = Σᵢ f(xᵢ*)·Δx  ·  Δx=(b−a)/n  ·  xᵢ*: left/right/midpoint sample point

Increase n: the Riemann sum converges to the true area. Negative values of f(x) are subtracted from the area. Watch the error go to zero.
