The Gram–Schmidt Process

Chapter 1 · Linear Vector Spaces · Orthogonalization

The Gram–Schmidt Process

We keep $u_1=v_1$. From $v_2$ we subtract its projection onto $u_1$:

$\mathrm{proj}_{u_1}v_2=\dfrac{\langle v_2,u_1\rangle}{\langle u_1,u_1\rangle}\,u_1$  ·  $u_2=v_2-\mathrm{proj}_{u_1}v_2$

Then $\langle u_2,u_1\rangle=0$ (orthogonal). Normalizing $e_i=u_i/\lVert u_i\rVert$ we obtain an orthonormal basis.
