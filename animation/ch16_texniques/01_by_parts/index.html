Integration by Parts

The product rule for derivatives: (uv)′=u′v+uv′  → integrating: ∫uv′dx = uv − ∫u′v dx.

∫u dv = uv − ∫v du  ·  Choice: u = the part that simplifies when differentiated, dv = the part that is easy to integrate

ILATE mnemonic: Inverse trig. > Logarithmic > Algebraic > Trigonometric > Exponential. Sometimes the rule has to be applied more than once.
