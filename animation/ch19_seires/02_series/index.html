Series — Partial Sums

A series Σaₙ converges if the sequence of partial sums Sₙ = a₁+...+aₙ converges.

Geometric series: Σrⁿ = 1/(1−r) if |r|<1  ·  Harmonic series Σ(1/n): diverges!

Tests: ratio test (|aₙ₊₁/aₙ|→L<1⟹convergence), root test, comparison test. Necessary condition: aₙ→0 (but NOT sufficient).
