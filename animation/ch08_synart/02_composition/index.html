Composition of Functions

The composition (f∘g)(x) = f(g(x)): first g is applied, then f.

Domain of f∘g: {x ∈ Dom(g) : g(x) ∈ Dom(f)}. In general f∘g ≠ g∘f.

Important: if g=f⁻¹ then f∘g = id. Error checking: verify that g(x) ∈ Dom(f) for every x.
