Mathematical Background

Chapter 0 · Mathematical Background

How to use them. The exercises are arranged by section of the chapter and, within each group, in increasing order of difficulty: BASIC INTERMEDIATE ADVANCED.

This is review material: every exercise is solved using only the tools of the present chapter, without derivatives and without integrals. Before you start computing, identify the structure of the expression; most solutions are short once the right formula is chosen. Every result is checked by substituting back into the data.

A. Number sets, inequalities and absolute values

First restrict the domain, then study the sign; the solution set is always written as an interval or a union of intervals.

0E.1 BASIC

Solve the double inequality $-3\le 2x+1<7$ and write the solution set as an interval.

0E.2 INTERMEDIATE

Solve the inequalities:

(α) $|2x-1|<|x+3|$,    (β) $\displaystyle \frac{x^2-4}{x+1}\le 0$.

Hint: in (α) both sides are nonnegative, so the inequality is preserved if you square them. In (β) factor the numerator and construct a sign table; note carefully which point is excluded.

0E.3 ADVANCED

Prove that for all $a,b,c\in\mathbb{R}$ we have $a^2+b^2+c^2\ge ab+bc+ca$ and determine when equality holds.

Hint: examine the sum $(a-b)^2+(b-c)^2+(c-a)^2$.

B. The coordinate plane and the straight line

Distance, midpoint and slope are enough to describe completely every line of the plane.

0E.4 BASIC

The points $A(-3,5)$ and $B(5,-1)$are given. Compute the length $|AB|$ and the coordinates of the midpoint $M$ of the segment $AB$.

0E.5 BASIC

Find the equation of the line through the point $(0,4)$ and parallel to the line $3x-y+2=0$.

0E.6 INTERMEDIATE

The points $A(1,1)$, $B(5,3)$ and $\Gamma(3,7)$are given. Show that the triangle $AB\Gamma$ is right-angled and compute its area.

Hint: compare the slopes of the sides or their lengths. At which vertex do you look for the right angle?

C. Vectors in the plane and in space

The inner product measures lengths and angles at the same time; almost every question reduces to it.

0E.7 BASIC

The vectors $\vec{u}=(3,-4)$ and $\vec{v}=(-1,2)$ of the plane and the vector $\vec{w}=(2,-3,6)$ of space are given. Compute:

(α) the vector $2\vec{u}-3\vec{v}$,    (β) the magnitudes $\|\vec{u}\|$ and $\|\vec{w}\|$,    (γ) the corresponding unit direction vectors.

0E.8 INTERMEDIATE

Find all the unit vectors of the plane that are perpendicular to $\vec{v}=(3,4)$.

Hint: write the required vector as $(a,b)$ and combine the perpendicularity condition with the condition $\|(a,b)\|=1$. How many solutions do you expect geometrically?

0E.9 INTERMEDIATE

Prove the parallelogram law: for all $\vec{u},\vec{v}$ of the plane or of space we have $\|\vec{u}+\vec{v}\|^2+\|\vec{u}-\vec{v}\|^2=2\|\vec{u}\|^2+2\|\vec{v}\|^2$.

Hint: use the relation $\|\vec{w}\|^2=\vec{w}\cdot\vec{w}$ and the distributivity of the inner product.

D. Algebraic formulas and factorization

Recognize the pattern (difference of squares, sum of cubes, perfect square) before carrying out any computation.

0E.10 BASIC

Factor completely in $\mathbb{R}$: (α) $x^3+27$,    (β) $4x^2-12x+9$,    (γ) $x^4-16$.

0E.11 BASIC

Simplify the expression $\displaystyle \frac{1}{x-1}-\frac{2}{x^2-1}$ for $x\neq\pm 1$.

0E.12 INTERMEDIATE

Find the constant term, that is, the term that does not contain $x$, in the expansion $\displaystyle \left(x+\frac{1}{x}\right)^{6}$ (for $x\neq 0$).

Hint: write the general term of the Binomial Theorem and require that the exponent of $x$ vanish.

E. Polynomial equations

The Rational Root Theorem for detecting the roots, Horner's method for lowering the degree.

0E.13 BASIC

Using Horner's method find the quotient and the remainder of the division of $p(x)=x^4-3x^3+2x-5$ by $x+1$.

Hint: do not forget the zero coefficient of $x^2$.

0E.14 INTERMEDIATE

Solve in $\mathbb{R}$ the equation $2x^3-x^2-8x+4=0$.

Hint: as an alternative to searching for rational roots, try grouping the terms in pairs.

0E.15 ADVANCED

The polynomial $p(x)=x^3+ax^2+bx-6$ has as roots the numbers $1$ and $-2$. Determine $a,b\in\mathbb{R}$ and find its third root.

Hint: two independent conditions follow from the relations $p(1)=0$ and $p(-2)=0$; the third root is then found by factorization.

F. Trigonometry

Pythagorean identity, quadrant and transformation formulas: these three cover the whole group.

0E.16 BASIC

If $\cos\theta=-\dfrac{4}{5}$ and $\theta\in\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2},\pi\right)$, compute $\sin\theta$, $\tan\theta$ and $\cos 2\theta$.

Hint: the quadrant determines the sign of the square root.

0E.17 INTERMEDIATE

Solve on the interval $[0,2\pi)$ the equation $2\sin^2 x+\sin x-1=0$.

Hint: with the substitution $t=\sin x$ a quadratic equation results; factor it and return to the variable $x$.

0E.18 ADVANCED

Prove the identity $\displaystyle \frac{\cos x-\cos 3x}{\sin 3x-\sin x}=\tan 2x$, for every $x$ with $\sin x\neq 0$ and $\cos 2x\neq 0$.

Hint: convert numerator and denominator into products using the sum-to-product formulas.

G. Complex numbers

Algebraic form for the operations, exponential form for powers and roots, the complex plane for the geometry.

0E.19 BASIC

Write in the form $a+bi$ the numbers $\displaystyle \frac{3-i}{1+2i}$ and $i^{2027}$.

Hint: for the quotient multiply numerator and denominator by the conjugate of the denominator; for the power use the periodicity of the powers of $i$.

0E.20 INTERMEDIATE

Find all the fourth roots of $z=-16$ in exponential and in algebraic form and represent them in the complex plane.

Hint: first write $-16$ in trigonometric form. How are the roots distributed on the circle?

0E.21 ADVANCED

Show that the set of $z\in\mathbb{C}$ that satisfy the condition $|z-1|=2\,|z+1|$ is a circle, and determine its center and its radius.

Hint: set $z=x+iy$, square both sides and complete the square in $x$.

H. Mixed problems

Exercises that combine several sections; here the method is not given, you choose it yourself.

0E.22 BASIC

Compute the value of the expression $\displaystyle \log_2 32-\log_2\sqrt{2}+\log_3\frac{1}{9}$.

0E.23 INTERMEDIATE

Starting from Euler's formula $e^{i\theta}=\cos\theta+i\sin\theta$ and the property $e^{i(\alpha+\beta)}=e^{i\alpha}\,e^{i\beta}$, prove the formulas $\cos(\alpha+\beta)=\cos\alpha\cos\beta-\sin\alpha\sin\beta$ and $\sin(\alpha+\beta)=\sin\alpha\cos\beta+\cos\alpha\sin\beta$.

Hint: carry out the multiplication and equate real and imaginary parts separately.

0E.24 ADVANCED

Consider the polynomial $p(x)=x^4+1$.

(α) Show that $p$ has no real roots.

(β) Find its four complex roots in exponential form.

(γ) By combining the conjugate roots in pairs, write $p(x)$ as a product of two quadratic trinomials with real coefficients.

Hint: in (γ) the product $(x-z)(x-\bar{z})$ is expressed in terms of $z+\bar{z}$ and $|z|^2$.
