Monotonicity and the Derivative

Chapter 11: Extrema

Learning objective: f'(x)>0 ⟹ increasing, f'(x)<0 ⟹ decreasing. The critical points (f'=0) are the candidates for extrema.

Monotonicity & the First Derivative Test

f is increasing on (a,b) if f′(x)>0 there, and decreasing if f′(x)<0.

Test: f′>0 ⟹ increasing  ·  f′<0 ⟹ decreasing  ·  f′=0 ⟹ critical point

Method: find the critical points (f′=0), then check the sign of f′ on each interval. A change of sign at a critical point ⟹ a local extremum.
