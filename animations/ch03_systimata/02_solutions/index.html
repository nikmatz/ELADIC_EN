Types of Solutions of a Linear System

A system Ax=b has: a unique solution (rank=n), infinitely many (rank<n, consistent) or none (rank(A)<rank([A|b])).

Rouché–Capelli theorem: the system is consistent ⟺ rank(A) = rank([A|b])

Geometrically in ℝ²: two lines intersect (unique solution), are parallel (no solution), or coincide (infinitely many).
