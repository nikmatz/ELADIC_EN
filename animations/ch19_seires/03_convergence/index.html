Geometric series: $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} r^n$. See how the partial sums $S_N=\sum_{n=0}^{N} r^n$ converge (or diverge).

The Geometric Series & the Ratio Test

$\sum r^n$ converges exactly when $|r|<1$, with sum:

$\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} r^n=\frac{1}{1-r}\quad(|r|<1)$

The ratio test gives $\lim|a_{n+1}/a_n|=|r|$: if $<1$ it converges, if $>1$ it diverges. For $|r|\ge1$ the terms do not tend to $0$, so the series diverges.
