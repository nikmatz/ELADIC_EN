Phase Portraits of the system x′ = A·x

The type of equilibrium is determined by the eigenvalues of A
How to read it: the gray arrows are the vector field (the "flow" of the system), the colored curves are solution trajectories. The sign of the real part of the eigenvalues decides the stability (convergence to or divergence from the origin), while the imaginary part produces the rotation (spiral/center).
