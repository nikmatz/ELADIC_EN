Linear Transformations of ℝ²

Every linear transformation T:ℝ²→ℝ² corresponds to a 2×2 matrix: T(x)=Ax.

Properties: T(u+v)=T(u)+T(v), T(λu)=λT(u). It preserves lines, ratios and the origin.

Types: rotation, scaling, reflection, shear. The matrix A determines the transformation COMPLETELY.
