The Unit Circle & Special Angles

The unit circle has center $(0,0)$ and radius $r=1$. For every angle $\theta$ the point $P$ has coordinates $(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)$.

$\sin\theta = $ ordinate $y$  ·  $\cos\theta = $ abscissa $x$  ·  $\tan\theta = $ length at $x=1$

$\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1$ (Pythagorean identity). The quantity $\tan\theta$ arises geometrically as the segment cut by the extension of the radius on the vertical tangent line $x=1$; that is why it blows up at $90^\circ,270^\circ$. Special values at $30^\circ,45^\circ,60^\circ,90^\circ$ (buttons).
