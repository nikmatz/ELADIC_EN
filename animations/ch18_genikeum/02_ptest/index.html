p-test: the improper integral $\int_1^{\infty}\frac{1}{x^{p}}\,dx$ converges if $p>1$ and diverges if $p\le1$. Move the slider for $p$.

Improper Integrals — the p-test

For $\int_1^{\infty} x^{-p}\,dx$:

$\int_1^{\infty}\frac{dx}{x^{p}}=\dfrac{1}{p-1}\ (p>1)\ ;\qquad =\infty\ (p\le1)$

The shaded region extends to infinity. If $p>1$ the “tail” decays fast enough for the total area to be finite.
