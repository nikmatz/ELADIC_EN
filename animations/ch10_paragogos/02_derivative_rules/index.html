Rules of Differentiation

Basic rules that spare us computing from the definition at every step.

(f+g)′=f′+g′  ·  (f·g)′=f′g+fg′  ·  (f/g)′=(f′g−fg′)/g²  ·  (f∘g)′=(f′∘g)·g′ [Chain rule]

Basic derivatives: (xⁿ)′=nxⁿ⁻¹, (eˣ)′=eˣ, (sin x)′=cos x, (cos x)′=−sin x, (ln x)′=1/x. The chain rule is fundamental for composite functions.
