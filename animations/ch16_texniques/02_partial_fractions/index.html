Partial Fraction Decomposition

For distinct real roots $r_1\ne r_2$:

$\dfrac{1}{(x-r_1)(x-r_2)}=\dfrac{A}{x-r_1}+\dfrac{B}{x-r_2},\quad A=\dfrac{1}{r_1-r_2},\ B=\dfrac{1}{r_2-r_1}=-A$

Useful for integration: $\int=\!A\ln|x-r_1|+B\ln|x-r_2|+C$. Graphically, the sum of the two simple fractions reproduces the original function.
